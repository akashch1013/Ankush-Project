# Proof and checkpoints

## Inspect the page as a reader

Check at fit-page size, inspect consequential detail, then read the complete page again. Follow the actual reading direction. Watch for competing entry points, ambiguous tails, covered expressions or contact points, small labels, weak contrast, colour-only distinctions and unwanted working labels. Inspect the outgoing panel beside the next opening when available.

Compare the composed page beside the selected master or sub-location image and the applicable design-manual example. Identify where the location’s colours and art treatment appear in the background, borders, embellishments and maths graphics that are present. At reading size, name the first thing the learner notices and check that it leads to the assigned question, diagram or working. If an ornament resembles a tick, cell, data point or answer cue, simplify or move it and proof again. Carry recurring swatches and their meanings across neighbouring pages in the same sequence.

Compare each exact block with its source, including punctuation, styling, paragraph order, inscriptions and maths. Recheck equipment state, light, character proportions, pose, physical sides and ownership across cuts. Verify counts, axes, intervals, units, exponents and answers directly. Approach an unanswered question as a learner. An attractive illustration or a correct answer in the notes cannot certify the depicted figure.

## Use the current checks

Apply the checklist assigned to this edition, including integrated manual pages if applicable. Capture the complete item text. Read wrapped sentences, tables and page breaks against their original. Accept the project's own IDs and length. For complex layouts, convert once to a checked UTF-8 JSON array of objects with `id` and `requirement`, retaining the original document and its fingerprint. Verify the first, last and every wrapped requirement. When the assignment has no formal checklist, review against its manuscript, design and delivery requirements. Identify these observations as project production checks.

Mark each applicable result with a concrete observation tied to the exact proof revision. Use the project's status names or Pass, Needs fix, Not checked and Not applicable. Explain exceptions. Verify links, actual fonts/glyphs, overset, crop and native PDF settings on the production route. Measure effective resolution at placement, then judge the smallest essential detail at intended viewing size. Apply a numerical resolution target only when the edition specifies it.

## Complete corrections and delivery

Fix the affected art or layout, re-export and inspect nearby elements and page turns. On a limited revision, preserve the rest of the page and compare both renders outside the edited area when useful. Keep author approval separate from internal review. Record whether the proof was actually exported in Adobe or produced as a reading preview, and which live checks are still pending.

Save the manuscript/manual/asset identities, placement plan, selected linked art, editable source, required redistributable fonts and licences or font requirements, proof revision, export evidence, unresolved items and outgoing state. On resume, reuse verified material and invalidate only pages whose inputs changed. Combine verified individual PDFs in requested manuscript order without rasterising or downsampling them.
