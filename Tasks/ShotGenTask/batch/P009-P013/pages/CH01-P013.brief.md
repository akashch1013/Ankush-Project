## Story beat

Tara lists the thief's sabotage across the fair. These are the problems the volunteers will meet in later chapters: changed number cards, mixed food labels, swapped costume parts, false repair reports, a hidden weight in a game, a turned Grand Pavilion, and power sent to the sealed launcher on the Moon Deck. By the time she found out, the thief and the Bell were gone. The mission has two parts that cannot be separated: repair ChandRush and recover the Bell before moonset.
