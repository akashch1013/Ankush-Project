## Story beat

The three volunteers are now complete. Navya, the planner, steps forward a second after Ahana and Vihaan. Vihaan makes a dry joke about volunteering before hearing the details. Then Tez explains what ChandRush is and the one rule it runs on, the Balance Rule. This page sets up the logic of the whole book: the fair works only when its numbers, patterns, shapes, measurements and chance are correct.
