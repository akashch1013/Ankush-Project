# CH01-P010 — Moon Mischief and the reader's role

**Book opening / prologue · Opening sequence E, "The Moon Bell theft and the mission" · V07 page 10 (was V06 p07)**
Location: the Old Blue Gate exterior, beside the emergency hatch. The page note also names Entry Court for the briefing, but all three panels here stay at the Gate.

## Story beat

Tez explains Moon Mischief: when something is mathematically wrong, ChandRush magic copies or exaggerates the mistake. Then he says the key line of the book's teaching contract: magic can show what is wrong, but it cannot do the maths. Finally he turns to the reader. The ChandRush Crew learns each idea with the team and uses it on the real repair.

Reading flow:
1. Tall explanation (PN01).
2. Spoken line (PN02).
3. Tez faces the reader (PN03 inset), with the reader call-out set on the page background.
