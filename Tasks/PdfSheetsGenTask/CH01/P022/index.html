Division

Division can be checked by multiplication. For example, 6 ÷ 2 = 3 because 3 × 2 = 6. Similarly, 12 ÷ 3 = 4 because 4 × 3 = 12. We say division and multiplication are inverse operations: one undoes the other.

The answer to a division is called the quotient. To find −6 ÷ 2, ask, “What number multiplied by 2 gives −6?”

Find the missing numbers

(a) (−6) ÷ 2 = □ is the same as −6 = □ × 2. What does □ stand for?

(b) (−12) ÷ 4 = ○ is the same as −12 = ○ × 4. What does ○ stand for?

Check your method
