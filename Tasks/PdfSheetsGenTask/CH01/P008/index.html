Example 2

Evaluate without a calculator: (a) 2 + 3 − 4 and (b) 10 − 7 − 6. You may use a calculator afterwards to check.

Let’s work it through

With only addition and subtraction, work from left to right. The answer from the first step becomes the starting number for the next.

(a) First 2 + 3 = 5. Now subtract 4 from that 5:

2 + 3 − 4 = 5 − 4 = 1

(b) First 10 − 7 = 3. Now subtract 6 from that 3:

10 − 7 − 6 = 3 − 6 = −3

For the last step, 3 − 3 gets us to zero; the remaining 3 steps take us to −3.
