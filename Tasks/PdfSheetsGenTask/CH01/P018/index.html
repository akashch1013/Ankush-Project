Two red warning lights shine on the control box. Tara keeps the lift stopped and opens the little panel below them.

Inside are four numbered setting wheels for the safety gate’s catches. Each catch needs the same small correction before it can release.

A fixed maintenance plate says TOTAL CORRECTION −8; FOUR EQUAL SETTINGS. The wheels have been left at +2, 0, −1 and +3.

These numbers adjust the catches, not the lift’s floor.
The car stays here. What equal setting belongs on each wheel so that all four give a total of minus eight?
