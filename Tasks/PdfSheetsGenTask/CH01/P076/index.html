Example 16

Evaluate (a) ∛8 + 2³, (b) ∛125 − 5³ and (c) ∛64 × 5 − 3³.

Let’s work it through

(a) The root and the cube are different instructions. ∛8 = 2, while 23 = 8:

∛8 + 23 = 2 + 8 = 10
10

(b) Here ∛125 = 5, while 53 = 125. Subtracting the bigger amount gives a negative answer:

∛125 − 53 = 5 − 125 = −120
−120

(c) Find the root and the cube first. Then multiply before subtracting:

∛64 × 5 − 33 = 4 × 5 − 27 = 20 − 27 = −7

The root bar covers 64 only. The × 5 stays outside it.

−7
Now you try

Evaluate (a) ∛64 × 5, (b) ∛216 × 3 − 5³ and (c) 5 × ∛343.

Check your method
(a) ∛64 × 5 = 4 × 5 = 20
(b) ∛216 × 3 − 53 = 6 × 3 − 125 = 18 − 125 = −107
(c) 5 × ∛343 = 5 × 7 = 35
The cubes at the back are still on the team.
