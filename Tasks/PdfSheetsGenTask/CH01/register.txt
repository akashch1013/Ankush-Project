## Spatial layout
The spatial reference is the existing ChandRush fair map used for Chapter 01. Left and right within Lantern Street are defined from Entry Court looking towards Food Street. The lift is near-left; the continuous bench is right. Facing the bench, timing is left, projector kits centre and the hook rack right. Farther along, the left mirror faces the right cube station; the moonline follows the right paving edge.
Street level 0 and lower store −8 belong to one lift shaft. Food Street joins Lantern Street through one shared shuttered threshold. At Old Blue Gate, left and right are defined from the Mumbai waiting lane facing the main leaves.
