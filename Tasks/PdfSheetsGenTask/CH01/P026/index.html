5 Do brackets change the answer

(a) Do [17 − (−7)] − 2 and 17 − [(−7) − 2] have the same value?

(b) From (a), what can you say about the order of subtraction?

Check your method

Brackets tell us which part to calculate first. The round brackets in (−7) keep a negative number together; the square brackets group a calculation.

In the first expression, calculate 17 − (−7) first:

[17 − (−7)] − 2 = [17 + 7] − 2 = 24 − 2 = 22

In the second expression, calculate (−7) − 2 first:

17 − [(−7) − 2] = 17 − (−9) = 17 + 9 = 26

(a) No: 22 ≠ 26. (b) Changing which subtraction we do first can change the answer. Keep the brackets and follow their grouping.
