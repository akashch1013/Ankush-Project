Example 11

Salim wants to pack 171 pencils, 27 erasers and 63 pens into gift packs. Every pack must contain the same number of pencils, the same number of erasers and the same number of pens, with nothing left over. Find the greatest number of packs and the number of each item in one pack.

Let’s work it through

Each kind of item must be shared equally, with none left over. So the number of packs must divide 171, 27 and 63 exactly. We want as many packs as possible: that means finding their HCF.

171= 1 × 171= 3 × 57= 9 × 19
27= 1 × 27= 3 × 9
63= 1 × 63= 3 × 21= 7 × 9

The common factors are 1, 3 and 9. The greatest is 9, so Salim can make 9 packs. Now share each kind of item among those 9 packs:

Pencils per pack:171 ÷ 9 = 19
Erasers per pack:27 ÷ 9 = 3
Pens per pack:63 ÷ 9 = 7

Each pack contains 19 pencils, 3 erasers and 7 pens. Every pack has that same mixture; the numbers 19, 3 and 7 do not have to match each other. Check the pencils, for example: 9 × 19 = 171.

Now you try

Josephine has 28 candies, 49 stickers and 35 magnets. She wants identical goodie bags with nothing left over. Find the greatest number of bags and how many of each item go in one bag.

Check your method

Factors of 28: 1, 2, 4, 7, 14, 28.

Factors of 49: 1, 7, 49. Factors of 35: 1, 5, 7, 35.

The common factors are 1 and 7, so the HCF is 7. She can make 7 bags:

28 ÷ 7 = 4candies per bag
49 ÷ 7 = 7stickers per bag
35 ÷ 7 = 5magnets per bag
