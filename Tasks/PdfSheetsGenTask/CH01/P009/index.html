What happens when the next number is negative

These two patterns will help us find out. In each column, the number after the operation sign goes down by 1 each time. Look at what happens to the answer, then fill the four blanks.

Addition pattern
7 + 3 = 10
7 + 2 = 9
7 + 1 = 8
7 + 0 = 7
Line 1:7 + (−1) = □
Line 2:7 + (−2) = □
Subtraction pattern
7 − 3 = 4
7 − 2 = 5
7 − 1 = 6
7 − 0 = 7
Line 3:7 − (−1) = □
Line 4:7 − (−2) = □
Check your method

The addition answers go 10, 9, 8, 7, 6, 5. We add one less each time, so the total is one less. Lines 1 and 2 are:

7 + (−1) = 6 = 7 − 1
7 + (−2) = 5 = 7 − 2

The subtraction answers go 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9. We subtract one less each time, so one more remains. Lines 3 and 4 are:

7 − (−1) = 8 = 7 + 1
7 − (−2) = 9 = 7 + 2

Notice the two jobs of a minus sign in 7 − (−2). The first − tells us to subtract. The second − belongs to the number −2. Brackets keep that negative number together.

Adding −2 has the same effect as subtracting 2. Subtracting −2 has the same effect as adding 2. We’ll take those ideas one at a time.
