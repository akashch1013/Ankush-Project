The helper pushes the cart to a long workbench against the right wall. At its left end, a little carved bird stands on a sliding platform.
Two brass catches hold the platform at its starting point. One catch is blue; the other is gold.
The blue lamp comes on and lifts the blue catch. The gold catch stays down, so the bird cannot move.
Later, the gold catch rises alone. The bird is still waiting.
I know. Long night.
Tez lowers his head towards it.
