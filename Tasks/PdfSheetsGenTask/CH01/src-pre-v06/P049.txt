[MathSection] 1.3 Divisibility Tests
Can we tell whether 452 divides exactly by 2 without doing the division? We can—and we’ll learn quick checks for other numbers too. First, let’s be clear about what “divides exactly” means.
24 ÷ 2 = 12, with no remainder. We say 24 is divisible by 2. But 7 ÷ 2 = 3.5, which is not a whole number, so 7 is not divisible by 2. We’ll use positive whole numbers in the tests that follow.
[MathSubsection] Divisible by 2
The positive multiples of 2 are 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, … . These are even numbers. Their final digits are always 0, 2, 4, 6 or 8. A number ending in any of these digits is divisible by 2.
Why does the last digit decide? Every complete ten splits into pairs: 10 = 2 × 5. In 452 = 45 × 10 + 2, we have 45 complete tens and 2 extra ones. The tens split into pairs, and the 2 extra ones make one more pair. So 452 divides exactly by 2.
452, 8204, 72436, 648360 and 12345678 are divisible by 2: they end in 2, 4, 6, 0 and 8. However, 69231 ends in 1 and 1234567 ends in 7, so neither is divisible by 2. Whole numbers that do not divide exactly by 2 are called odd numbers; pairing them leaves one over.
