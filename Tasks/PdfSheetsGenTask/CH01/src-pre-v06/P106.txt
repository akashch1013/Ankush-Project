## Scene CH01 11D The question interrupted
[SourceLocator] Production instructions are non-printing. Exact reader text is assigned beneath each visual beat.
[SceneInstruction] Setting. Lantern Street. Broad pale paving curves between blue-painted stall walls under tall indigo arches. From Entry Court, the lift is near-left and the timing/supply bench follows the right wall. Farther on the left is the mirror alcove, opposite the right-side cube plinth and final control. Food Street shutters close the far bend. The narrow moonline follows the paving’s physical right edge. Same full-moon night after the joint repair. Golden arch light fills Lantern Street; the moonline is continuous. Warm work lamps remain, with cool moonlight on roofs and the mirror. This repair does not restore Tez’s stripes or ring the Moon Bell.
[SceneInstruction] View. Return inside to a medium looking along restored Lantern Street towards the still-closed shutters at the far bend.
[SceneInstruction] Cast and staging across the scene. Ahana turns to Tara and asks who Veer is. Tara is beside her, about to respond. Vihaan and Navya turn towards the sudden crash. Tez’s head swivels towards the shutters. The helper waits behind with the cart.
[SceneInstruction] Props and state progression. Ahana still holds the evidence sleeve. Before this movement she retrieves the work lamp; Tara retrieves her staff from its supported position at the cube wall. Lantern Street remains repaired, handle down. Shutters have not yet risen in this still.
[SceneInstruction] Planning requirements. The spoken question precedes the crash. Do not add Tara’s Chapter 02 explanation here. No visible villain causes the sound.
