(b) Each pair uses neighbouring whole numbers. The difference between their squares equals the sum of those two numbers: 4² − 3² = 4 + 3 = 7, for example.
Look at a 4-by-4 square with its 3-by-3 corner removed. The remaining L-shaped border has 4 tiles along the bottom and 3 more up the side. That gives 4 + 3 = 7; we count the corner tile only once.

(c) 200 and 199 are neighbours, so:
[Equation] 200² − 199² = 200 + 199 = 399
