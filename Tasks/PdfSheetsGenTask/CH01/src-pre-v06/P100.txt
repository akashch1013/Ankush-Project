## Scene CH01 10G Rejoining the track
[SourceLocator] Production instructions are non-printing. Exact reader text is assigned beneath each visual beat.
[SceneInstruction] View. Low medium from the lane side of the open joint. Show hands, lamp and both track ends without covering the working area.
[SceneInstruction] Cast and staging across the scene. Tara holds Ahana’s lamp in one hand and lights Navya’s work; her staff remains against the wall. Navya kneels on stable paving and uses a small tool from her pouch to rejoin the track ends. Ahana stands beside the control, watching; Vihaan keeps the loose cover out of the path. Tez stays behind them, away from the opening; the helper watches the fittings.
