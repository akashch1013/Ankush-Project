[MathExample] 3 Find the missing digit
The five-digit number 2345□ is divisible by 3 but not by 9. What could the missing digit be?
[AnswerLead] Check your method
Add the known digits first: 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 = 14. The missing digit can be 0 to 9, so the full digit sum can be 14 to 23.
The multiples of 3 in that range are 15, 18 and 21. They give these possibilities:
[Equation] 14 + 1 = 15;   14 + 4 = 18;   14 + 7 = 21
But 18 is divisible by 9, so the digit 4 is not allowed. The possible digits are 1 or 7. They give 23451 and 23457, with digit sums 15 and 21: divisible by 3, but not by 9.

[MathExample] 4 Check Mary’s rule
Mary checks whether a number is divisible by 4 by checking whether its digit sum is divisible by 4. How would you convince her that this rule is incorrect?
[AnswerLead] Check your thinking
Give Mary a number for which her test fails. For example, 12 ÷ 4 = 3, so 12 is divisible by 4. But its digit sum is 1 + 2 = 3, which is not divisible by 4.
Mary’s rule was meant to work every time. The number 12 shows that it does not. For the correct 4-test, check the last two digits.
