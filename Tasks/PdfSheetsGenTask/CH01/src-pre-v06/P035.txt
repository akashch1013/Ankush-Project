# Sequence 06 Identical projector kits
[SourceLocator] Production sequence reference CH01-06. The following location descriptions are retained from V16.
### Lantern Street Projector-Kit Bench, Centre
[SceneInstruction] The centre of the pale right-wall bench holds two labelled supply boxes, padded empty kit boxes and one sample board. The blue/gold timing lamps are farther left, and the sealed hook rack is to the right. A short lantern-test rail is fixed above.
[SceneInstruction] Labels read 45 BLUE GLASS LENSES and 75 BRASS RINGS. Establish the greatest possible number of identical kits before teaching. A completed three-lens/five-ring head and exact sample kit belong only after T1.2B. Three rings mount the lenses and two secure the supports. The full stock need not be individually laid out; one sample and closed kit boxes are enough. Do not show an already-solved three-and-five recipe in the setup.
[SceneInstruction] Required views. Stock-label close-up; overhead sample-kit view; medium handover to wooden crews. These are parts of the Lantern Street bench, not a new workshop.
## Scene CH01 06A The stock for the lantern heads
[SourceLocator] Production instructions are non-printing. Exact reader text is assigned beneath each visual beat.
[SceneInstruction] Setting. Lantern Street projector-kit bench. The centre of the continuous right-wall pale bench holds two stock boxes, padded kit boxes and a sample board. Timing lamps remain farther left, the sealed-hook rack farther right. The short lantern-test rail is fixed above against the blue wall, under warm task light. Same full-moon night in Mumbai. Warm fixed work lamps and the portable brass lamp make faces and hands readable; cool moonlight edges roofs and canopy gaps. Main lantern arches remain dark unless this scene explicitly changes them.
[SceneInstruction] View. Medium at the centre of the right-wall bench specified under Setting in Scene CH01 06A. The blue and gold timing fittings remain farther left, the sealed-hook rack farther right, and a short test rail above.
[SceneInstruction] Cast and staging across the scene. The broad helper opens the two supply boxes. Ahana places an empty padded kit box between them with her left hand; she holds the work lamp clear with her right. Navya stands to the right with a blank packing sheet; Vihaan studies the two stock labels. Tara explains the identical-kit requirement, staff supported against the wall so she can indicate both stocks. Tez waits under the clear edge of the bench, outside the work area.
[SceneInstruction] Props and state progression. Box labels: 45 BLUE GLASS LENSES and 75 BRASS RINGS. Empty padded kit boxes and a blank sample board are available. Do not individually scatter all 120 items. LIFT and RHYTHM white; remaining three indicators dark.
[SceneInstruction] Planning requirements. The empty kit and stock labels must be visible. Do not show a completed projector head, a three-and-five recipe or fifteen prepared kits before T1.2B. The maximum number of identical kits is the stated question.

### Visual beat CH01 06A A The available stock
[SceneInstruction] Moment and staging. The helper opens the two boxes on the centre bench. Ahana lights the 45-lens and 75-ring labels; Navya holds a blank packing sheet, Vihaan examines the stocks, Tara stands beside them with her staff leaned securely against the wall. Tez waits below the clear bench edge. No finished sample is visible.
[PrintLabel] CAPTION TEXT — PRINT ONLY THIS
[CaptionCopy] The helper opens the next two boxes. One holds small blue glass lenses. The other holds brass rings that shine gold in the lamplight. Together they make the light-projecting heads for the lantern arches. A complete head uses lenses to shape the light and rings to hold the head and its supports together. Tara wants identical kits so several wooden crews can work at once.
