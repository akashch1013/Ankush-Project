# Sequence 08 The square mirror
[SourceLocator] Production sequence reference CH01-08. The following location descriptions are retained from V16.
### Lantern Street Square Mirror Recess
[SceneInstruction] A shallow square brass frame stands just above the paving in an alcove on the far-left wall. A blue rear wall, a warm lamp on the left and an open canopy gap identify it. A padded tray and opaque tile crate sit outside the walking lane.
[SceneInstruction] The inventory is 81 equal, unbroken square mirror tiles. The helper empties the mislaid arrangement before the mathematical layout. The exact result is one flat 9 × 9 grid, reflecting one continuous full moon. The inner brass rim carries a V hidden in dust until the arches light. Keep that rim, lamp, crate and paving seam consistent. A diagram supplies the count; a wide image need not reveal every tile.
[SceneInstruction] Required views. Medium empty-frame setup; high three-quarter exact grid after teaching; separate Tez reflection view; later rim-and-V close-up from the same frame.
## Scene CH01 08A Broken patches of moonlight
[SourceLocator] Production instructions are non-printing. Exact reader text is assigned beneath each visual beat.
[SceneInstruction] View. Cross-lane medium towards the far-left mirror alcove. Retain the blue rear wall, left warm lamp, open canopy gap, shallow brass frame and paving seam. A separate preliminary still shows crooked tiles; the main working still shows the helper clearing them.
[SceneInstruction] Cast and staging across the scene. Tara directs the trio across the lane, staff in her right hand. Ahana carries the work lamp. Navya looks into the frame; Vihaan looks at the scattered reflections. Tez stays near Ahana. The helper fixes the loose frame and transfers misplaced tiles onto the padded tray outside the walking route.
[SceneInstruction] Props and state progression. All 81 tiles are unbroken and belong to this one frame. The opaque crate and padded tray stand clear of the lane. The frame is fixed at a shallow tilt towards the canopy opening; that mounting does not change in later views. The V remains concealed in dust.
[SceneInstruction] Planning requirements. Do not copy the older location image’s floor-mounted open square or its exposed 3-by-27 tile array. V16 specifies a shallow frame in this wall alcove. No completed 9-by-9 arrangement before teaching.
