## Scene CH01 01B Tez meets the cart handle
[SourceLocator] Production instructions are non-printing. Exact reader text is assigned beneath each visual beat.
[SceneInstruction] Setting. Lantern Street. Broad pale paving curves between blue-painted stall walls under tall indigo arches. From Entry Court, the lift is near-left and the timing/supply bench follows the right wall. Farther on the left is the mirror alcove, opposite the right-side cube plinth and final control. Food Street shutters close the far bend. The narrow moonline follows the paving’s physical right edge. Same full-moon night in Mumbai. Warm fixed work lamps and the portable brass lamp make faces and hands readable; cool moonlight edges roofs and canopy gaps. Main lantern arches remain dark unless this scene explicitly changes them.
[SceneInstruction] View. Medium low view along the entrance paving specified under Setting in Scene CH01 01A, with the near-left lift wall and one dark arch as anchors. Freeze the instant after Tez bumps the stationary handle.
[SceneInstruction] Cast and staging across the scene. Tez stands squarely on four paws, ears lifted in embarrassed dignity, facing the handle. Ahana catches the handle with her left hand and raises the lamp with her right; she looks down at him with concern. Vihaan watches from behind her left shoulder, amused but not mocking. Navya stands to Ahana’s right, attentive. Tara remains one step behind with her staff.
[SceneInstruction] Props and state progression. The empty cart rests against the stall wall outside the route, its handle projecting into the lamp’s circle. Keep Tez’s navy scarf and moon clasp; several of his blue stripes, especially the established shoulder patches, are pale grey.
[SceneInstruction] Planning requirements. Tez speaks to the group; Ahana addresses Tez and delivers her final line lightly to him and the obstructing cart. Balloon tails point to mouths without covering eyes. No comic injury.
