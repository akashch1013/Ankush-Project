[MathSubsection] Four operations on integers
On a number line, adding a positive amount moves right; subtracting a positive amount moves left:
[Equation] 6 + 4 = 10;   8 − 5 = 3
Adding a negative moves left; subtracting a negative moves right:
[Equation] 2 + (−3) = 2 − 3 = −1
[Equation] 2 − (−3) = 2 + 3 = 5

For multiplication and division of non-zero numbers, different signs give a negative answer; same signs give a positive answer:
[Equation] 3 × (−2) = −(3 × 2) = −6
[Equation] −3 × 2 = −(3 × 2) = −6
[Equation] 64 ÷ (−4) = −(64 ÷ 4) = −16
[Equation] (−64) ÷ 4 = −(64 ÷ 4) = −16
For matching signs: (−3) × (−2) = 6 and (−12) ÷ (−3) = 4.
Check a division by multiplying the answer by the divisor. For example, (−16) × (−4) = 64 checks 64 ÷ (−4) = −16.
