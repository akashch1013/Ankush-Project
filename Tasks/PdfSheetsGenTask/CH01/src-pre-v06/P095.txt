[MathExample] 9 Work without a calculator
Calculate (a) 13 + 2 × 5², (b) (2³ − 12) × 4 and (c) 6 − (10 − 4²).
[AnswerLead] Check your method
[Equation] (a) 13 + 2 × 5² = 13 + 2 × 25 = 13 + 50 = 63
[Equation] (b) (2³ − 12) × 4 = (8 − 12) × 4 = (−4) × 4 = −16
[Equation] (c) 6 − (10 − 4²) = 6 − (10 − 16) = 6 − (−6) = 12
In (c), working inside the brackets still means doing 4² before 10 − 16. The last step subtracts a negative, so 6 − (−6) = 6 + 6 = 12.
