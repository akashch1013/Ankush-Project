## Scene CH01 04D What the five lights mean
[SourceLocator] Production instructions are non-printing. Exact reader text is assigned beneath each visual beat.
[SceneInstruction] Setting. Lantern Street. Broad pale paving curves between blue-painted stall walls under tall indigo arches. From Entry Court, the lift is near-left and the timing/supply bench follows the right wall. Farther on the left is the mirror alcove, opposite the right-side cube plinth and final control. Food Street shutters close the far bend. The narrow moonline follows the paving’s physical right edge. Same full-moon night in Mumbai. Warm fixed work lamps and the portable brass lamp make faces and hands readable; cool moonlight edges roofs and canopy gaps. Main lantern arches remain dark unless this scene explicitly changes them.
[SceneInstruction] View. Medium beside the loaded cart facing towards the right-wall bench, with the far control board visible only as a distant landmark.
[SceneInstruction] Cast and staging across the scene. Ahana looks towards the single lit indicator and asks Tara. Tara indicates the far board, then the box with a blue-and-gold lamp painted on its lid. Vihaan and Navya turn towards the right bench. The helper holds the cart handle ready to move. Tez stands beside Ahana.
[SceneInstruction] Props and state progression. One blue-and-gold-lamp-marked supply box on the cart; no new standalone machine. LIFT white, other indicators dark. All five characters’ belongings remain with them; Tara has her staff again.
[SceneInstruction] Planning requirements. Print the box description as caption; the painted lamp is an illustration, not an invented word label. The helper moves the cart specified under Props and state progression in Scene CH01 04D to the continuous right-wall bench specified under Setting in Scene CH01 04D.

The children follow her gaze; the portable lamp remains in Ahana’s hand as specified in Visual beat CH01 04D A, and the staff remains in Tara’s hand as specified in Visual beat CH01 04D B. Keep the long dialogue readable in the later layout.
