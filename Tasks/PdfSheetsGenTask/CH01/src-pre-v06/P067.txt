[MathSubsection] Practice 1D
Watch which number the raised 2 or root sign belongs to. Some questions ask for several separate calculations.
[MathExample] 1 Evaluate
(a) 3² (b) 7² (c) 5² + 6² (d) 8 × 9² − 5²
[AnswerLead] Check your method
[Equation] (a) 3² = 3 × 3 = 9
[Equation] (b) 7² = 7 × 7 = 49
[Equation] (c) 5² + 6² = 25 + 36 = 61
(d) This time we have squares, multiplication and subtraction together. Work out 9² and 5² first, then multiply by 8, then subtract:
[Equation] 8 × 9² − 5² = 8 × 81 − 25 = 648 − 25 = 623
The raised 2 belongs to 9, so square 9 before multiplying by 8. Squaring 8 × 9 would answer a different question.
