[AnswerLead] Check your method
(a) A cube’s side must fit exactly along all three lengths: 30 cm, 18 cm and 12 cm. Find their HCF.
Factors of 12 are 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12. Those that also divide both 18 and 30 are 1, 2, 3 and 6. The greatest is 6, so each cube has side length 6 cm.
(b) Count cubes along, across and up:
[Equation] Along the 30 cm length: 30 ÷ 6 = 5
[Equation] Along the 12 cm depth: 12 ÷ 6 = 2
[Equation] Along the 18 cm height: 18 ÷ 6 = 3
[Equation] Total: 5 × 2 × 3 = 30 cubes
[SmallCaption] Five by two by three cubes
