[AnswerLead] Check your method
Find the edge of each complete cube from its volume:
[Equation] Large cube edge: ∛64 = 4 cm
[Equation] Removed cube edge: ∛8 = 2 cm
Look at the marked horizontal edge. The original full width was 4 cm; the removed corner uses 2 cm of that width. The remaining part is:
[Equation] 4 − 2 = 2 cm
So the missing length is 2 cm. Subtracting the volumes, 64 − 8 = 56 cm³, would find the remaining space, not this edge length.
