[MathSubsection] Practice 1E
Here the questions mix squares, cubes and their roots. Look closely at the signs before you start: 4³ asks for a cube, while ∛64 asks for a cube root.
[MathExample] 1 Evaluate
(a) 8³ (b) 12³ (c) 4³ (d) 5³
[AnswerLead] Check your method
[Equation] (a) 8³ = 8 × 8 × 8 = 64 × 8 = 512
[Equation] (b) 12³ = 12 × 12 × 12 = 144 × 12 = 1728
[Equation] (c) 4³ = 4 × 4 × 4 = 16 × 4 = 64
[Equation] (d) 5³ = 5 × 5 × 5 = 25 × 5 = 125

[MathExample] 3 Evaluate
(a) 8² + ∛27 (b) ∛729 − √25
(c) ∛125 − ∛8 + 3³ (d) 2² + 4³ × ∛8
[AnswerLead] Check your method
[Equation] (a) 8² + ∛27 = 64 + 3 = 67
[Equation] (b) ∛729 − √25 = 9 − 5 = 4
[Equation] (c) ∛125 − ∛8 + 3³ = 5 − 2 + 27 = 3 + 27 = 30
[Equation] (d) 2² + 4³ × ∛8 = 4 + 64 × 2 = 4 + 128 = 132
In (d), multiply 64 × 2 first; then add the waiting 4.

[MathExample] 4 Evaluate
(a) 7³ − ∛8 (b) √64 − ∛64
(c) 2² × 4³ − ∛512 (d) 2³ − ∛343 − ∛27
[AnswerLead] Check your method
[Equation] (a) 7³ − ∛8 = 343 − 2 = 341
[Equation] (b) √64 − ∛64 = 8 − 4 = 4
[Equation] (c) 2² × 4³ − ∛512 = 4 × 64 − 8 = 256 − 8 = 248
[Equation] (d) 2³ − ∛343 − ∛27 = 8 − 7 − 3 = 1 − 3 = −2
In (b), both roots contain 64, but their instructions differ: 8 × 8 = 64, whereas 4 × 4 × 4 = 64.
