By 9: Add the digits, then check whether their sum is divisible by 9. Use the same split: 42 = 36 + (4 + 2) = 36 + 6. The 36 divides by 9, but the remaining 6 does not. So 42 passes the 3-test and fails the 9-test. The shortcut works for larger numbers too: 9, 99 and 999 all divide by 9, leaving the digits to check.
[Equation] 4536: 4 + 5 + 3 + 6 = 18 = 9 × 2 → yes
[Equation] 125793: 1 + 2 + 5 + 7 + 9 + 3 = 27 = 9 × 3 → yes
[Equation] 149: 1 + 4 + 9 = 14 → no
[Equation] 62341: 6 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 1 = 16 → no

By 10: A number made of complete tens has 0 ones: 1230 = 123 × 10. So numbers ending in 0 pass. 1230 and 4570 pass; 1235 and 1489 fail.
By 25: Count in twenty-fives: 25, 50, 75, 100, then 125, 150, 175, 200. Four lots make a complete hundred: 4 × 25 = 100. The endings repeat: 25, 50, 75, 00.
So 6725, 3550, 8675 and 14500 pass. The endings 45 in 17345 and 20 in 68520 do not work.
By 100: Complete hundreds end in 00: 6800 = 68 × 100. So 6800 and 451200 pass; 6210 and 7505 fail. One final zero is enough for the 10-test; here we need two.
