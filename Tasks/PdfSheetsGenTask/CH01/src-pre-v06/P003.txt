## Scene CH01 01C The knock in the lift bay
[SourceLocator] Production instructions are non-printing. Exact reader text is assigned beneath each visual beat.
[SceneInstruction] View. Street-side medium facing the recessed shaft in the left wall. Show the warm wall lamp above the blue-and-brass controller on the shaft’s right.
[SceneInstruction] Cast and staging across the scene. Tara stands nearest the fixed lamp and turns it up with her left hand, staff upright in her right. ## Scene CH01 01D The helper below
[SourceLocator] Production instructions are non-printing. Exact reader text is assigned beneath each visual beat.
[SceneInstruction] Setting. Lower store and upper street landing of the same Lantern Street goods lift. The first view looks down to the guarded −8 store with its warm lamp and timber edge. The single empty car stays above at street level 0. Tara’s speaking views are on the street paving beside the closed brass guard, blue-and-brass controller and fixed wall lamp; no character changes levels. Same full-moon night in Mumbai. Warm fixed work lamps and the portable brass lamp make faces and hands readable; cool moonlight edges roofs and canopy gaps. Main lantern arches remain dark unless this scene explicitly changes them.
[SceneInstruction] View. Begin with a steep guarded view down to the lower store. Then cut back to a street-level medium beside the fixed lamp and controller for Tara’s explanation. For Visual beat CH01 01D C, retain the street-level camera and framing specified in Visual beat CH01 01D B.
[SceneInstruction] Cast and staging across the scene. The broad wooden helper waits beside its cart on the guarded −8 landing, tapping the metal box. Tara and the children remain on safe street paving above. In the two street-level speaking images, Tara stands behind the children, staff in her right hand, with her left hand free to gesture. Ahana holds the lamp; Vihaan stands by the position strip, Navya by the slots and Tez behind their feet. The helper remains below and outside those street-level crops.
[SceneInstruction] Props and state progression. The supply cart is entirely on the lower landing. The empty car remains above at 0 and need not be included in this downward crop. Show the −8 landing mark, the lower safety barrier, timber store edge and warm lamp.
[SceneInstruction] Planning requirements. The helper view carries narration only. Tara is visible for both speaking beats, with each balloon tail at her mouth. Keep Tara’s position, framing and staff hand as specified in Visual beat CH01 01D B; apply the free-hand gesture, gaze and expression changes specified in Visual beat CH01 01D C. Preserve the helper’s painted eyes, wood grain, brass joints and separate wooden thumb.
