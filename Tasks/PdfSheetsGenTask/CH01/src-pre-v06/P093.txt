Finally fit 7. Try the shared multiples of 1800 in order. The extra number after each multiplication is the remainder:
[Equation] 1800 = 7 × 257 + 1;   3600 = 7 × 514 + 2
[Equation] 5400 = 7 × 771 + 3;   7200 = 7 × 1028 + 4
[Equation] 9000 = 7 × 1285 + 5;   10800 = 7 × 1542 + 6
[Equation] 12600 = 7 × 1800
12600 is the first with no remainder. We checked every earlier shared multiple, so it is the smallest number that works.
It still fits the other three: 12600 ÷ 8 = 1575, 12600 ÷ 9 = 1400 and 12600 ÷ 100 = 126. The 100-test also covers 2, 4, 5, 10 and 25; the 9-test covers 3; passing 2 and 3 covers 6.
