[MathExample] 3 Evaluate
(a) √100 (b) √144 (c) √81 − √49 (d) 3 + √36 + 4²
[AnswerLead] Check your method
[Equation] (a) √100 = 10, because 10² = 100
[Equation] (b) √144 = 12, because 12² = 144
[Equation] (c) √81 − √49 = 9 − 7 = 2
[Equation] (d) 3 + √36 + 4² = 3 + 6 + 16 = 9 + 16 = 25
The bar of a root sign shows what belongs under it. In (d), only 36 is under √; the separate 3 and 4² stay outside.
