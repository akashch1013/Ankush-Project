[MathExample] 2 Evaluate
(a) −11 + 5 − 8 (b) 120 − 80 − 50 (c) −15 × 8
(d) 64 ÷ (−16) (e) (−82 − 18) ÷ 4 − 12
(f) [32 − (−49 ÷ 7) × 4] + 80
[AnswerLead] Check your method
[Equation] (a) −11 + 5 − 8 = −6 − 8 = −14
[Equation] (b) 120 − 80 − 50 = 40 − 50 = −10
[Equation] (c) −15 × 8 = −(15 × 8) = −120
[Equation] (d) 64 ÷ (−16) = −(64 ÷ 16) = −4
[Equation] (e) (−82 − 18) ÷ 4 − 12 = −100 ÷ 4 − 12 = −25 − 12 = −37
(f) There are brackets inside brackets. Start with −49 ÷ 7 = −7. Inside the square brackets, multiply −7 × 4 before subtracting:
[Equation] [32 − (−49 ÷ 7) × 4] + 80
[Equation] = [32 − (−7) × 4] + 80
[Equation] = [32 − (−28)] + 80
[Equation] = [32 + 28] + 80
[Equation] = 60 + 80 = 140
The subtraction of −28 becomes addition of 28. Keeping the brackets until that point makes the two minus signs easier to follow.
