[MathSubsection] Lowest common multiple and highest common factor
The LCM is the smallest positive shared multiple. For 6 and 8:
[Equation] Multiples of 6: 6, 12, 18, 24, 30, 36, 42, 48, …
[Equation] Multiples of 8: 8, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48, …
The common multiples begin 24, 48, …, so LCM(6, 8) = 24. “Lowest” and “least” mean the same thing here.
The HCF is the greatest positive shared factor. Factors of 6 are 1, 2, 3, 6. Factors of 8 are 1, 2, 4, 8. Their common factors are 1 and 2, so HCF(6, 8) = 2.
For a first shared total or return time, look for an LCM. For the greatest number of equal packs, or the largest piece that fits exactly, look for an HCF.

[MathSubsection] Divisibility tests
For 2, check for a last digit of 0, 2, 4, 6 or 8. For 3 or 9, add the digits and check whether the sum divides exactly by 3 or 9. For 4, check the last two digits; for 8, the last three. For 5, the last digit must be 0 or 5. For 6, pass both the 2-test and 3-test.
For 7, double the last digit and subtract it from the remaining number; repeat if needed. For 10, the last digit must be 0. For 25, the last two digits must be 00, 25, 50 or 75. For 100, the last two digits must be 00.
