[MathExample] 5 Use divisibility tests
Check whether each number is divisible by 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 25 and 100.
(a) 504  (b) 12345678
[AnswerLead] Check your method
(a) The digit sum of 504 is 5 + 0 + 4 = 9. For 7, use 50 − 2 × 4 = 42, which is divisible by 7.
(b) The digit sum of 12345678 is 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 6 + 7 + 8 = 36. Its 7-test goes:
[Equation] 1234567 − 2 × 8 = 1234551
[Equation] 123455 − 2 × 1 = 123453
[Equation] 12345 − 2 × 3 = 12339
[Equation] 1233 − 2 × 9 = 1215
[Equation] 121 − 2 × 5 = 111
[Equation] 11 − 2 × 1 = 9
9 is not divisible by 7, so 12345678 fails that test.
