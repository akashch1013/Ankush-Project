[MathSubsection] Finding the side from the square
Now reverse the question. If a square has 25 little tiles in all, how many go along one side? We want a number that multiplies by itself to give 25. Since 5 × 5 = 25, the side has 5 tiles.
That reverse operation is a square root. The symbol √ means “square root”.
[Equation] √25 = 5 because 5² = 25
[Equation] √4 = 2 because 2² = 4
[Equation] √100 = 10 because 10² = 100
[Equation] √169 = 13 because 13² = 169
For a square of area 25 cm², the side length is √25 = 5 cm. Notice how the answer returns to a length in centimetres.
There is one sign detail to watch. Both 2 × 2 and (−2) × (−2) equal 4, but √4 asks for the positive answer, 2. The √ symbol gives the root that is positive or zero; it does not give −2 here.
On a calculator: find the √ key and enter the number under the root. For √121, the display should give 11. Check it by squaring: 11 × 11 = 121.

[MathExample] Example 13
Evaluate (a) 5² + 7² and (b) 9² − 35.
[AnswerLead] Let’s work it through
Calculate each square before adding or subtracting.
[Equation] (a) 5² + 7² = 5 × 5 + 7 × 7 = 25 + 49 = 74
[Equation] (b) 9² − 35 = 9 × 9 − 35 = 81 − 35 = 46
In (a), 5² + 7² means two separate squares. Adding 5 + 7 first would change the calculation.
