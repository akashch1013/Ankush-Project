The knock comes again. This time they can hear where it comes from. There’s a lift in the left wall. Tara turns up a fixed lamp beside it.
Far below, the wooden helper in the blue waistcoat stands beside its cart of supplies. It is tapping a metal box to attract attention.
That helper took the supplies down before the trouble began.
Someone sent the empty lift back up and changed the return instructions. The helper’s still down there with the supplies.
