# Sequence 05 The moving lantern bird
[SourceLocator] Production sequence reference CH01-05. The following location descriptions are retained from V16.
### Lantern Street Timing Bench, Left End
[SceneInstruction] A pale workbench stands against the right-hand blue wall beneath a warm lamp. A carved bird is fixed to one short sliding platform. A blue catch and lamp stand on the left, a gold catch and lamp on the right; a beat strip sits between them.
[SceneInstruction] The catches independently rise every four and six beats. Both must be open before the bird platform moves. The wrong start is 6; the corrected first common start is 12. The neighbouring kit cartons remain farther right on this same bench. In an exact insert the time strip must show the shared twelfth beat, not suggest that the two cycles themselves change.
[SceneInstruction] Required views. Medium of the two catches holding the bird; separate exact timing strips; still with counter 12 and both catches raised; bird at its end stop.
## Scene CH01 05A Two catches hold the bird
[SourceLocator] Production instructions are non-printing. Exact reader text is assigned beneath each visual beat.
[SceneInstruction] View. Medium from the lane facing the left end of the right-wall bench. Blue catch and lamp are physically on the left, gold on the right, with the beat strip between.
[SceneInstruction] Cast and staging across the scene. The helper parks the cart alongside the bench, outside the walkway. Navya stands nearest the strip; Vihaan is beside the blue catch.
