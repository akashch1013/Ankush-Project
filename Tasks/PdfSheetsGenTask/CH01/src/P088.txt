[MathExample] 3 Fire alarms
Three fire alarms go off every 3 minutes, 5 minutes and 8 minutes. They first go off together at 10 a.m. When will all three next go off together?
[AnswerLead] Check your method
We need the first shared wait, so use an LCM. Multiples of both 3 and 5 are 15, 30, 45, 60, 75, 90, 105, 120, … . The first of these also divisible by 8 is 120:
[Equation] 120 ÷ 3 = 40;   120 ÷ 5 = 24;   120 ÷ 8 = 15
None of the earlier times in that list divides by 8 exactly. So we wait 120 ÷ 60 = 2 hours. 10 a.m. + 2 hours = 12 p.m., or noon.
