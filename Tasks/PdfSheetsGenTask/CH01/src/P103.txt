# Sequence 11 The signature and the next street
[SourceLocator] Production sequence reference CH01-11. The following location descriptions are retained from V16.
### Lantern Street Square Mirror Recess
[SceneInstruction] A shallow square brass frame stands just above the paving in an alcove on the far-left wall. A blue rear wall, a warm lamp on the left and an open canopy gap identify it. A padded tray and opaque tile crate sit outside the walking lane.
[SceneInstruction] The inventory is 81 equal, unbroken square mirror tiles. The helper empties the mislaid arrangement before the mathematical layout. The exact result is one flat 9 × 9 grid, reflecting one continuous full moon. The inner brass rim carries a V hidden in dust until the arches light. Keep that rim, lamp, crate and paving seam consistent. A diagram supplies the count; a wide image need not reveal every tile.
[SceneInstruction] Required views. Medium empty-frame setup; high three-quarter exact grid after teaching; separate Tez reflection view; later rim-and-V close-up from the same frame.
### Old Blue Gate Exterior Waiting Lane
[SceneInstruction] Two enormous weathered blue timber doors stand between pale-stone piers. Brass ribs and moon-shaped openings decorate the doors. A round full-moon ornament hangs above. Seen from outside, a small service door is cut into the left door leaf. A bench is on the right and a planter on the left.
[SceneInstruction] The separate equipment exit is farther left beyond the stone pier. It is not the child-height door in the large leaf. Children aged eight to thirteen wait in contemporary urban casual clothing. Use a few foreground groups and a suggested crowd; do not render three hundred individually countable people. Ira, Kabir and the girl in green remain identifiable. Tara does not meet the trio outside. The Bell is never mounted on the Gate arch.
[SceneInstruction] Required views. Arrival wide from the lane; bench/planter medium; invitation close-up; low Tez doorway view. Gate ornaments and invitations have bright, fading and restored-light states without changing their shapes.
### Food Street Master from Lantern Street
[SceneInstruction] A covered lane beneath saffron and teal awnings opens beyond Lantern Street’s shutters. The recipe and cooking counter is on the left; jugs and the juice urn are on the right. The near packing bench has a side door to Return Walk. A round two-tray loading table sits in the central clear lane.
[SceneInstruction] The far opening leads to the Parade Ground. The initial obstruction is one crooked cart with a bowl under its wheel. The food helper wears a saffron apron and ivory cap; two smaller assistants deliver food. The main cooking surface is behind the counter, beyond the children’s hands. The sixteen-cup trolley waits at the packing end. No human cooks or hidden extra kitchen are introduced.
[SceneInstruction] Required views. Threshold wide; separate recipe, drinks and packing counter views; central loading-table medium. The waiting Gate crowd is shown in its own cutaway, never behind the cooking counter.
