[MathExample] Example 5
Evaluate (a) 2 + (−3) − (−5), (b) 10 − (−7) + (−5) and (c) 6 + (−7) − (−1).
[AnswerLead] Let’s work it through
First replace each addition or subtraction of a negative with the change it means. Then work from left to right.
(a) Adding −3 becomes subtracting 3; subtracting −5 becomes adding 5:
[Equation] 2 + (−3) − (−5) = 2 − 3 + 5 = −1 + 5 = 4
(b) Here −(−7) becomes +7, and +(−5) becomes −5:
[Equation] 10 − (−7) + (−5) = 10 + 7 − 5 = 17 − 5 = 12
(c) Keep the starting 6 unchanged:
[Equation] 6 + (−7) − (−1) = 6 − 7 + 1 = −1 + 1 = 0
