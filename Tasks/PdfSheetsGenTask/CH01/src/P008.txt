[MathExample] Example 2
Evaluate without a calculator: (a) 2 + 3 − 4 and (b) 10 − 7 − 6. You may use a calculator afterwards to check.
[AnswerLead] Let’s work it through
With only addition and subtraction, work from left to right. The answer from the first step becomes the starting number for the next.
(a) First 2 + 3 = 5. Now subtract 4 from that 5:
[Equation] 2 + 3 − 4 = 5 − 4 = 1
(b) First 10 − 7 = 3. Now subtract 6 from that 3:
[Equation] 10 − 7 − 6 = 3 − 6 = −3
For the last step, 3 − 3 gets us to zero; the remaining 3 steps take us to −3.

[MathExample] Now you try
Evaluate without a calculator: (a) 6 + 3 − 5, (b) 7 − 4 − 5 and (c) −3 − 4 + 5. You may use a calculator afterwards to check.
[AnswerLead] Check your method
[Equation] (a) 6 + 3 − 5 = 9 − 5 = 4
[Equation] (b) 7 − 4 − 5 = 3 − 5 = −2
[Equation] (c) −3 − 4 + 5 = −7 + 5 = −2
In (c), the first move takes −3 down to −7. Adding 5 then brings −7 back up to −2.
