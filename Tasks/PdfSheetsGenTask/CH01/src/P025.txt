[MathSubsection] Practice 1A
Try each question before uncovering the working. Use the number line if a negative sign makes you hesitate.
[MathExample] 1 Evaluate
(a) 7 + (−3) (b) −2 + (−3) (c) 17 − (−2)
(d) −2 − (−5) (e) −5 − (−7) (f) 0 − (−9)
[AnswerLead] Check your method
[Equation] (a) 7 + (−3) = 7 − 3 = 4
[Equation] (b) −2 + (−3) = −2 − 3 = −5
[Equation] (c) 17 − (−2) = 17 + 2 = 19
[Equation] (d) −2 − (−5) = −2 + 5 = 3
[Equation] (e) −5 − (−7) = −5 + 7 = 2
[Equation] (f) 0 − (−9) = 0 + 9 = 9
In (d), only −(−5) changes to +5. The starting number stays −2.

[MathExample] 2 Evaluate
(a) 4 + 9 − 6 (b) 17 − 7 − 8
(c) 4 − 5 + 6 − 7 (d) 6 − 8 − 9 + 2
[AnswerLead] Check your method
Follow addition and subtraction from left to right:
[Equation] (a) 4 + 9 − 6 = 13 − 6 = 7
[Equation] (b) 17 − 7 − 8 = 10 − 8 = 2
[Equation] (c) 4 − 5 + 6 − 7 = −1 + 6 − 7 = 5 − 7 = −2
[Equation] (d) 6 − 8 − 9 + 2 = −2 − 9 + 2 = −11 + 2 = −9
