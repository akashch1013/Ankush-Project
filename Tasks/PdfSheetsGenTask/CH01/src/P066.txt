[MathExample] What happens with a negative number
Use a calculator to try √(−4). What happens? Can you explain why?
[AnswerLead] Check your thinking
The calculator usually reports an error. Why? No number on our number line has square −4. A positive number squared is positive; a negative number squared is also positive; and zero squared is zero.
For example, (−2) × (−2) = 4, not −4. So √(−4) has no real-number answer. The calculator is flagging the mathematical problem, not asking for fresh batteries.
