## Scene CH01 11C A little hope outside
[SourceLocator] Production instructions are non-printing. Exact reader text is assigned beneath each visual beat.
[SceneInstruction] Setting. Old Blue Gate exterior waiting lane in Mumbai. Two enormous weathered blue timber doors stand between pale-stone piers, with brass ribs, moon-shaped openings and one round full-moon ornament above. Seen from outside, the small service door is in the left door leaf, the planter is left and the bench right. The separate equipment exit lies farther left beyond the pier. Same full-moon night, simultaneous with the restored street. Ira’s invitation and the round Gate ornament brighten against the Mumbai lane. The blue doors stay closed; nearby warm lamps keep the waiting children readable.
[SceneInstruction] View. Independent exterior cutaway facing the Old Blue Gate from the Mumbai lane. Retain the tall blue doors, pale piers, round moon ornament, left planter and right bench specified under Setting in Scene CH01 11C.
[SceneInstruction] Cast and staging across the scene. Ira in her pale powder-blue jacket holds up her invitation and shows it to Kabir, who leans slightly towards it in his rust hoodie. They then look up at the brightening ornament; separate stills may show these two gazes. The green-waistcoated Gate helper stands by the small service door. Modern child visitor groups wait nearby, including the green-hoodie girl with her friends. The trio, Tara and Tez are inside and not visible here.
[SceneInstruction] Props and state progression. Ira’s same silver crescent invitation glows again. The full-moon ornament brightens. Main Gate doors remain closed; this is not admission or the welcome ring. Kabir has his slim notebook; he is not yet counting an admitted group.
[SceneInstruction] Planning requirements. No off-frame inside dialogue over this cutaway. The outside children see a hopeful change but do not know about the V or Veer. Do not place the exterior crowd behind the solid interior map wall.
