[SourceLocator] Production instructions are non-printing. Exact reader text is assigned beneath each visual beat.
[SceneInstruction] Setting. Lower store of the Lantern Street goods lift. The guarded landing is at −8 below the same street shaft, with a warm lamp and timber store edge. The single lift car arrives level with this landing. The upper street remains 0; the blue controller and the children stay above. Same full-moon night in Mumbai. [SceneInstruction] Planning requirements. Keep the cart and helper fully supported. Loading does not imply the lift is moving.
