## Continuity at the chapter opening
The trio met in Mumbai and volunteered outside the Old Blue Gate. They are now inside with Tara and Tez. Their silver invitations bear blue volunteer seals. The Moon Bell has been stolen from its Entry Court frame; the dusty ivory cover is retained there. A Gate helper saw a covered trolley leave but did not identify its contents. Pip has already introduced Ira and Kabir to the messenger system. Neither a culprit’s present-day face nor the meaning of the dust has been revealed.
Ahana carries the brass work lamp Tara gave her. Her cream sketchbook and ordinary pencil are in her navy backpack. The lift is at street level 0, with the broad helper and one supply cart waiting below at −8. Tez has several faded pale-grey stripes. This chapter repairs a street but does not ring the Bell, open attractions, restore all his stripes or begin guest admission.
