The five-indicator wall control and large down-pull handle are on its right. A separate flat brass cover in the paving at the plinth’s front-right corner hides the right-edge moonline joint. First four indicators white, CUBE dark. The final handle is raised; the brass floor cover remains closed and its disconnection unseen.

## Scene CH01 09B Four layers of sixteen
[SourceLocator] Production instructions are non-printing. Exact reader text is assigned beneath each visual beat.
[SceneInstruction] View. Exact layer diagram followed by a medium of the helper building on the plinth specified under Setting in Scene CH01 09B. Keep the canopy post and square base as anchors.
[SceneInstruction] Cast and staging across the scene. Vihaan explains the layer, Navya adds the four-layer total. Ahana lights the base from the side. The helper places the cubes; Tara supervises. Tez watches from clear paving, away from the stacked blocks.
[SceneInstruction] Props and state progression. One layer has exactly 4 rows × 4 columns = 16 cubes. Four equal layers form one solid 4 × 4 × 4 cube = 64. Layer plans represent the same blocks, not spare stock.
[SceneInstruction] Planning requirements. This follows all T1.5 teaching and Practice 1E. Include hidden cubes in the total. Do not label the 2-by-2-by-2 or 3-by-3-by-3 teaching models as the story lantern.
