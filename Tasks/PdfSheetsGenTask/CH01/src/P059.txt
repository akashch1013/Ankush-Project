[MathExample] 5 Help John find a test
John wants a divisibility test for 15. Is there one? If so, explain it to him.
[AnswerLead] Check your thinking
Yes. A number must be divisible by both 3 and 5. Check that its digit sum is divisible by 3 and that its final digit is 0 or 5.
For example, 345 ends in 5 and has digit sum 3 + 4 + 5 = 12, so it passes both tests. Check: 345 ÷ 15 = 23. But 350 ends in 0 and has digit sum 3 + 5 + 0 = 8. It fails the 3-test, so it is not divisible by 15.
