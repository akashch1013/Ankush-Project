# Sequence 10 The street light and the broken connection
[SourceLocator] Production sequence reference CH01-10. The following location descriptions are retained from V16.
### Lantern Street Cube Plinth, Handle and Moonline Joint
[SceneInstruction] A low square plinth under an indigo canopy stands opposite the mirror. The five-indicator wall control and large down-pull handle are on its right. One flat brass cover lies in the paving at the plinth’s front-right corner, over the moonline joint.
[SceneInstruction] Sixty-four small lantern cubes form one solid 4 × 4 × 4 block between the square base and top frame. The failed state is uneven narrow towers. An exact layer diagram shows sixteen cubes, then four layers. The final handle stays separate from the floor cover. Under that cover are two disconnected track ends, screws and black dust; there is no hidden second switch. The joint repair, not another calculation, makes the final light flow.
[SceneInstruction] Required views. Medium plinth; exact single layer and complete cube inserts; hand-on-handle still; low disconnected/connected joint views; result wide belongs to Lantern Street.
### Lantern Street Master from Entry Court
[SceneInstruction] A broad pale-paved lane curves between blue-painted stall walls beneath tall indigo lantern arches. The lift bay is near-left; a pale timing and supply bench runs along the right wall. Farther on, a square mirror recess is on the left, opposite a cube-lantern plinth and final control. Food Street shutters close the far bend.
[SceneInstruction] The narrow silver moonline follows the paving’s right edge to a flat joining plate near the cube plinth. Five indicators beside the final handle read LIFT, RHYTHM, SUPPLIES, MIRROR and CUBE. Work lamps light the tasks before the main arches are restored. Intermediate lights do not mean the entire street is lit. The last state has golden arches and a continuous moonline. The repaired street remains repaired in every later return.
[SceneInstruction] Required views. Initial wide from Entry Court; separate reverse from the cube end; final restored wide. Fine mirror/cube counts are not required in distant views.
