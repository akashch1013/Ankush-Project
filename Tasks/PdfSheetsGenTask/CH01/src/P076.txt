[MathExample] Example 16
Evaluate (a) ∛8 + 2³, (b) ∛125 − 5³ and (c) ∛64 × 5 − 3³.
[AnswerLead] Let’s work it through
(a) The root and the cube are different instructions. ∛8 = 2, while 2³ = 8:
[Equation] ∛8 + 2³ = 2 + 8 = 10
(b) Here ∛125 = 5, while 5³ = 125. Subtracting the bigger amount gives a negative answer:
[Equation] ∛125 − 5³ = 5 − 125 = −120
(c) Find the root and the cube first. Then multiply before subtracting:
[Equation] ∛64 × 5 − 3³ = 4 × 5 − 27 = 20 − 27 = −7
The root bar covers 64 only. The × 5 stays outside it.
