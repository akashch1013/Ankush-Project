One possible set of answers (a) 4 : 3 = (4 × 3) : (3 × 3) = 12 : 9 (b) 6 : 8 = (6 ÷ 2) : (8 ÷ 2) = 3 : 4 (c) 6.4 : 1.6 = (6.4 × 10) : (1.6 × 10) = 64 : 16 64 : 16 = (64 ÷ 8) : (16 ÷ 8) = 8 : 2 (d) 0.01 : 0.02 = (0.01 × 100) : (0.02 × 100) = 1 : 2 Other equivalent ratios are correct too. This question asks for an equivalent ratio, so 8 : 2 is acceptable in (c); it does not have to be simplified to 4 : 1. END PANEL CH03-PAGE051-PANEL01 |
