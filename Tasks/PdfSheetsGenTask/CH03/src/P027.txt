(a) Find the ratio of Tim’s working hours to Peter’s. (b) Find the ratio of Tim’s salary to Peter’s. (c) Explain why the salaries are fair. Check your method (a) 20 : 12 = (20 ÷ 4) : (12 ÷ 4) = 5 : 3 (b) 100 : 60 = (100 ÷ 20) : (60 ÷ 20) = 5 : 3 (c) The ratios match. Tim receives $100 ÷ 20 = $5 per hour, and Peter receives $60 ÷ 12 = $5 per hour. They receive equal pay per hour for the same kind of work. END PANEL CH03-PAGE027-PANEL02 |
