For a sharing question, ask yourself: is the given amount the whole total, or just one share? Let that decide your first calculation. Revision Question 1 Write each ratio in its simplest form. (a) 25 : 35     (b) 8 : 120     (c) 9 m : 30 m     (d) 60 L : 85 L (e) 200 cm : 30 cm     (f) 5 kg : 1000 kg     (g) 7.8 : 4.8 Check your method (a) 25 : 35 = (25 ÷ 5) : (35 ÷ 5) = 5 : 7 (b) 8 : 120 = (8 ÷ 8) : (120 ÷ 8) = 1 : 15 (c) 9 m : 30 m = 9 : 30 = (9 ÷ 3) : (30 ÷ 3) = 3 : 10 (d) 60 L : 85 L = 60 : 85 = (60 ÷ 5) : (85 ÷ 5) = 12 : 17 (e) 200 cm : 30 cm = 200 : 30 = (200 ÷ 10) : (30 ÷ 10) = 20 : 3 (f) 5 kg : 1000 kg = 5 : 1000 = (5 ÷ 5) : (1000 ÷ 5) = 1 : 200 (g) First make both terms whole numbers, then simplify: 7.8 : 4.8 = (7.8 × 10) : (4.8 × 10) = 78 : 48 78 : 48 = (78 ÷ 6) : (48 ÷ 6) = 13 : 8 END PANEL CH03-PAGE050-PANEL01 |
