END NON-PRINTING CARRIED PRODUCTION CONTEXT ORIGINAL PANEL CONTENT 2. Search the Internet to find out how the golden ratio is used in everyday life. Try the search “golden ratio design documented example”. Choose an example, name where you found it, and explain what the designer used the ratio for. Look for the designer’s own organisation, a museum or a university, rather than copying an attractive diagram without checking it. One researched example Architect Le Corbusier used the golden ratio in a system of measurements called the Modulor. It helped him choose proportions for buildings. His foundation describes this use in its “Corbusean vocabulary” page. This is a documented use in design, rather than a guess based only on how a building looks. You can investigate another example. Record its name, the website and the evidence of deliberate use. Not every rectangle or spiral follows the golden ratio; measuring and checking are part of the investigation. Research source for the sample: Fondation Le Corbusier, “Corbusean vocabulary”, section “Modulor”, accessed 19 October 2026: https://www.fondationlecorbusier.fr/en/thematic-folder/corbusean-vocabulary/. The sample is supplied as one possible research result; no student search or observation is claimed. END PANEL CH03-PAGE047-PANEL01 |
