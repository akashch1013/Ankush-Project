END NON-PRINTING CARRIED PRODUCTION CONTEXT ORIGINAL PANEL CONTENT 3.1 Introduction to Ratio Let’s make the comparison easier to read. Eddy takes 10 shots at goal and scores 3 goals. Put the quantity named first on the left: shots at goal : goals scored = 10 : 3 Read 10 : 3 as “ten to three”. The two dots, called a colon, separate the numbers we are comparing. Those 3 goals came from the 10 shots; they are not 3 extra shots. A newspaper has 4 pages of sports news and 9 pages of entertainment news. Sports comes first in the comparison, so: sports pages : entertainment pages = 4 : 9 This kind of comparison is called a ratio. A ratio can compare two or more quantities. If we call two quantities A and B, we write their ratio as A : B. The letters tell us which quantities we mean. END PANEL CH03-PAGE006-PANEL01 |

Let’s work it through Say the names in order: Ali, then Ying. Now write their masses in that same order. Both masses are in kilograms, so we can compare the numbers directly. Ali : Ying = 52 kg : 47 kg = 52 : 47 Once the units match, we leave them out of the ratio. If we reverse the names, we must reverse the numbers too: Ying : Ali = 47 : 52. The numbers have not changed places for fun; the question has changed. END PANEL CH03-PAGE006-PANEL02 |
