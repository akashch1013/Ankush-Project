[MScene] Scene CH03 06C Two problems at once
[MMeta] DO NOT PRINT — Setting: Location CH03 L04 Gallery and simultaneous yard action. Upper-right gallery overlooks the same work bay. Near stair has a fixed handrail; the gallery’s far door opens to the rear walkway and has a bolt on its far side. Veer’s drive control is beside the gallery rail; ground stop is below. Keep upper and lower actions in separate views. The broad helper stays below; the parade helper remains at the stop during Tara’s first pursuit. One plain escort is sent towards the remote passage end; that instruction is not an already-completed seal of every route.
[MMeta] Cast and positions. Tara starts up the near stair; Veer retreats along the upper gallery. Parade helper holds stop below; broad helper remains at the restrained float. Trio and clown on the yard’s safe edge.
[MMeta] Props and continuity. Use separate views for stair and trolley. The clown’s trolley carries cushions over the costume box; it is light enough for Ahana to steady at its side handle. Its short loading board slopes towards the work bay but ends outside the blocked wheel area. It cannot enter the visitor lane or strike the float before being stopped.
