TEACHING COPY BEGINS — Production notes remain non-printing 3.2 Equivalent Ratios Look at the wheel groups. Each motorcycle has 2 wheels and each car has 4. We are counting all the wheels, including car wheels that a side-on picture might hide.  DO NOT PRINT — Figure reference V07. The following caption sentence is reader-facing. Count wheels, rather than vehicles. Production note V07 — Teaching figure: three outlined motorcycle groups with exactly two wheel symbols per group and three car groups with exactly four wheel symbols per group. Label each group Motorcycle or Car; all wheels must be individually visible. The totals are six and twelve, but vehicle counts are three and three. Use the current ChandRush colour and design style wherever it supports understanding. If that style would reduce clarity, retain the teaching colours, contrast and mathematical design required here. Mathematical accuracy and comprehension take priority. motorcycle wheels : car wheels = (3 × 2) : (3 × 4) = 6 : 12 For every 6 motorcycle wheels there are 12 car wheels. We can describe the same comparison with smaller numbers. The next activity will show what stays the same. END PANEL CH03-PAGE016-PANEL01 |
