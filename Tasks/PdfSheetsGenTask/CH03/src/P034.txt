[MScene] Scene CH03 03C Inside the costume box
[MMeta] Cast and positions. Clown crouches at his trolley’s low box; trio watch from the bench side. Ira and Kabir stay behind the viewing rope. Tara returns towards the work rail.
[MMeta] Props and continuity. The box is beneath a fixed upper cushion rack. Its low hinged lid can open without lifting the secured cushions. It has a simple catch, not a lock; the later jolt can release an incompletely caught lid. Trolley brake is applied on level paving.
