[MScene] Scene CH03 07F A bench blocks the path
[MMeta] DO NOT PRINT — Setting: Location CH03 L06 Moon Square board and fixed paving. From the Parade Arch: Clock Tower and Report Court left, Games Lane right, Grand Pavilion ahead. Pale paving surrounds low stage and three seating areas. Curved blue screen shelters one waiting group. Board and its accessible stop stand beside fixed outer paving, with a fixed shelf for the register. A folding bench can obstruct an inner path but never the preserved outer exit lane. First forty and next twenty-two make sixty-two guests; board stays at 40 after theft. Thirty are already seated, twelve by board, twenty behind screen. Ira and Kabir belong to twelve, girl in green and friends to twenty. No new signs displaying these subgroup answers yet.
[MMeta] Cast and positions. Tara reaches the board on the safe outer paving; Ira and Kabir near its path. Ahana beside them, Navya and Vihaan just behind. Tez stays on fixed paving. Other guests remain in their three waiting areas with seating helpers.
[MMeta] Props and continuity. Board display remains 40. Actual guest groups are 30 seated, 12 by board including Ira and Kabir, 20 behind blue screen including girl in green and her friends. Those counts are continuity data, not new visible labels or Chapter 04 solution. No actual child is missing.
