[MScene] Scene CH03 06B Stop the unfinished float
[MMeta] DO NOT PRINT — Setting: Location CH03 L04 Gallery and simultaneous yard action. Upper-right gallery overlooks the same work bay. Near stair has a fixed handrail; the gallery’s far door opens to the rear walkway and has a bolt on its far side. Veer’s drive control is beside the gallery rail; ground stop is below. Keep upper and lower actions in separate views. The broad helper stays below; the parade helper remains at the stop during Tara’s first pursuit. One plain escort is sent towards the remote passage end; that instruction is not an already-completed seal of every route.
[MMeta] Cast and positions. Veer at the gallery control; Tara at the ground stop lever. Parade helper beside Tara. Broad helper beside the blocked float. Trio stay on safe stand side; clown beside costume trolley; guests behind rope.
[MMeta] Props and continuity. No one rides the float. Final strap is unfitted. The control starts only the drive and opens the rehearsal gate; it does not release blocks or temporary support. Ground stop cuts drive and is held down until the safe test.
