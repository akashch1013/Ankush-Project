(a) Find the ratio of students to teachers in School A. (b) Find the ratio of students to teachers in School B. (c) Which school would you prefer to attend as a student? Explain your answer. Check your method (a) School A: 800 : 40 = (800 ÷ 40) : (40 ÷ 40) = 20 : 1 (b) School B: 1600 : 90 = (1600 ÷ 10) : (90 ÷ 10) = 160 : 9 (c) I would choose School B for its lower number of students per teacher. Compare both schools using 9 teachers: School A: 20 : 1 = (20 × 9) : (1 × 9) = 180 : 9 School B: 160 : 9 For 9 teachers, School B has 160 students compared with School A’s 180.
