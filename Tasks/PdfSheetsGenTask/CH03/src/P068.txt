[MScene] Scene CH03 07A The theft they do not see
[MMeta] DO NOT PRINT — Setting: Location CH03 L05 Counting arch and rear staff walkway. Far arch leads from the protected lane into Moon Square. Front reader and one silver slotted strip share a casing fixed at Navya’s waist height on the ground-level arch pier. Its rear cover opens into a recess hidden from the yard, accessible around the pier from safe paving. Upper rear walkway connects the bolted gallery door to a separate ticket-operated staff door. Existing near staff stair links walkway and recess: Veer descends to steal the strip, then returns upstairs before revealing it. This stair and the other approach serve Tara’s second pursuit without crossing the guest lane. Keep the same casing, slot and rear cover in every view. Writing shelf and message perch remain at the arch base.
[MMeta] Cast and positions. Veer alone first on the upper rear walkway, then in the ground-level recess behind the counting-arch pier, reached by the existing near staff stair. Tara and children remain in the yard off-frame, attending to the float and clown.
[MMeta] Props and continuity. Reader-only cutaway. The first forty have already been counted. Second twenty-two have not yet crossed. The rear cover exposes the one installed strip. Veer’s ticket stays in his inner coat pocket; walking stick in his right hand until rested for two-handed work. No clown clothes are stolen in Chapter 03. The single casing stays fixed at Navya’s waist height above ground paving. Veer uses its rear cover from the same ground recess that Navya later reaches from the safe side.
