They shared their payment in the ratio 8 : 6. Was the payment fair? Let’s work it through Here we’ll treat fair payment as the same pay for each hour of the same kind of work. Then the payment should be shared in the same ratio as the hours. hours, Thiru : John = 15 : 9 = (15 ÷ 3) : (9 ÷ 3) = 5 : 3 payment, Thiru : John = 8 : 6 = (8 ÷ 2) : (6 ÷ 2) = 4 : 3 The simplest ratios are 5 : 3 and 4 : 3. They do not match, so the payment was not fair on an equal-pay-per-hour basis. A share of 5 : 3 would match their working time. END PANEL CH03-PAGE021-PANEL01 |
