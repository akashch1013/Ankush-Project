Let’s work it through Simplify each ratio, keeping its order, then compare the results: 2 : 8 = (2 ÷ 2) : (8 ÷ 2) = 1 : 4 3 : 12 = (3 ÷ 3) : (12 ÷ 3) = 1 : 4 Both describe 1 part of the first quantity for 4 parts of the second, so they are equivalent. A fraction calculator would show 2/8 = 1/4 and 3/12 = 1/4 too. Your turn after Example 6 Determine whether 3 : 12 and 2 : 10 are equivalent ratios.
