[MScene] Scene CH03 05A Thirty ribbons to share
[MMeta] Cast and positions. Trio at a safe ribbon bench beside the model stand. Clown brings the ribbon box on the pedestrian side. Helpers change wheels behind the rail; Tara supervises there. Ira and Kabir stay at the viewing rope.
[MMeta] Props and continuity. Use an empty-drive, blocked float during wheel work. The thirty ribbons are initially plain; blue and gold are their intended colours. The ribbon box is distinct from the costume box beneath the trolley. Before this scene, the clown brings his light costume trolley along the clear near-side outer route to the ribbon stand, parks it level beside the short loading board and applies its brake. Its box stays closed and cushions secured. The near-side route remains clear for the children.
