END NON-PRINTING CARRIED PRODUCTION CONTEXT ORIGINAL PANEL CONTENT Revision Question 7 Jane worked for 2 full days and received $70. Alice worked for 3 full days and received $100. Were their salaries fair? Explain your answer. Check your method Assume their full working days were equally long and they did the same kind of work. Fair pay on that basis would mean the same amount for each day. working days, Jane : Alice = 2 : 3 pay, Jane : Alice = 70 : 100 = (70 ÷ 10) : (100 ÷ 10) = 7 : 10 The ratios 2 : 3 and 7 : 10 are not equivalent. Jane’s pay is $70 ÷ 2 = $35 per day. At that rate, Alice should receive 3 × $35 = $105, not $100. So their salaries were not fair on an equal-pay-per-day basis. END PANEL CH03-PAGE053-PANEL01 |

Lucy concludes that there are 4 apples and 5 oranges in the bag. Explain whether she is correct. If she is incorrect, what should her conclusion be? Check your method Lucy’s numbers are possible, but they are not the only possibility. A bag with 8 apples and 10 oranges also fits: 8 : 10 = (8 ÷ 2) : (10 ÷ 2) = 4 : 5 The ratio tells us “4 apples for every 5 oranges”. It does not tell us how many groups of 4 and 5 are in the bag. We need more information, such as the total number of fruits, to find the actual counts. END PANEL CH03-PAGE053-PANEL02 |
