END NON-PRINTING CARRIED PRODUCTION CONTEXT ORIGINAL PANEL CONTENT Example 5 Write each ratio in its simplest form: (a) 8 : 14 and (b) 14 : 4. Let’s work it through (a) Both 8 and 14 divide exactly by 2: 8 : 14 = (8 ÷ 2) : (14 ÷ 2) = 4 : 7 The terms 4 and 7 have no common factor greater than 1. That is our simplest ratio. (b) Both terms divide by 2 again: 14 : 4 = (14 ÷ 2) : (4 ÷ 2) = 7 : 2 The first term can be larger than the second. Keep their order. A calculator with a fraction feature can check this. Enter 8 as the top number and 14 as the bottom number. If it displays the simplified fraction 4/7, write the ratio as 4 : 7. For 14/4, the simplified fraction is 7/2, giving 7 : 2.
