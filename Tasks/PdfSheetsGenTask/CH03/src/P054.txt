If you disagree, how would you change the statement? Explain your answer. Check your method No. The 2 and 3 are the denominators, not the amounts being compared. Multiply both fractions by 6 to get whole-number terms: 1/2 × 6 = 6/2 = 3;     1/3 × 6 = 6/3 = 2 So 1/2 : 1/3 = 3 : 2, not 2 : 3. A quick size check helps: 1/2 is larger than 1/3, so the first term in our simplified ratio should be larger too. END PANEL CH03-PAGE054-PANEL01 |
