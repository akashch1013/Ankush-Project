[MScene] Scene CH03 04A A model to measure by
[MMeta] Cast and positions. Tara and the broad and parade helpers on the working side. Trio and Tez on the safe side of the low work stand. Ira and Kabir behind the adjacent viewing rope.
[MMeta] Props and continuity. The float is still blocked and temporarily strapped. Candidate wheels are held in a rack behind the rail; children cannot lift them. Model body 60 cm, actual body 300 cm, model wheel diameter 8 cm and model strap path 30 cm. No matching-part answers yet. Before arriving here, Ahana carries the rolled measuring tape from the costume bench and places it on this safe work stand. It remains there until CH03 06A D; her hands are free for the intervening work.
