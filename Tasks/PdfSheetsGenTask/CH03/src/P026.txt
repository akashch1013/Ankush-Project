Check your method (a) The first ratio is already 2 : 5. The second is 15 : 6 = (15 ÷ 3) : (6 ÷ 3) = 5 : 2. They are not equivalent; the order differs. (b) 8 : 4 = (8 ÷ 4) : (4 ÷ 4) = 2 : 1 32 : 16 = (32 ÷ 16) : (16 ÷ 16) = 2 : 1 Both simplify to 2 : 1, so they are equivalent. END PANEL CH03-PAGE026-PANEL02 |
