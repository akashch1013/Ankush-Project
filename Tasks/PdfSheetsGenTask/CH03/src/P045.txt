END NON-PRINTING CARRIED PRODUCTION CONTEXT ORIGINAL PANEL CONTENT Practice 3C Question 5 Car A is advertised as using 3 litres of petrol for 38 km. Car B is advertised as using 2 litres for 25 km. Which car would you choose? Explain your answer. Check your method For fuel efficiency, compare the distances travelled on the same amount of petrol. Finding the distance for 1 litre gives: Car A: 38 ÷ 3 = 12 2/3 km per litre Car B: 25 ÷ 2 = 12 1/2 km per litre For Car A, 3 × 12 = 36, leaving 2 km to share among 3 litres. That gives another 2/3 km per litre. For Car B, 2 × 12 = 24, leaving 1/2 km per litre. The whole parts are both 12. Compare the extra fractions using sixths: 2/3 = (2 × 2)/(3 × 2) = 4/6;     1/2 = (1 × 3)/(2 × 3) = 3/6 Since 4/6 is greater than 3/6, Car A travels farther per litre. I would choose Car A for its better advertised fuel efficiency. We are choosing on the given fuel figures; the question gives no other features to compare. END PANEL CH03-PAGE045-PANEL01 |
