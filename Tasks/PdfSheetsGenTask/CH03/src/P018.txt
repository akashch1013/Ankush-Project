Mathematical accuracy and comprehension take priority. The white part fills the same share of every strip, and the red part fills the same share too. In each strip, the red length is twice the white length. More dividing lines have not changed that comparison. Write three other ratios equivalent to 1 : 2. What do you observe about their numbers? One possible answer 1 : 2 = (1 × 5) : (2 × 5) = 5 : 10 1 : 2 = (1 × 7) : (2 × 7) = 7 : 14 1 : 2 = (1 × 10) : (2 × 10) = 10 : 20 The second term is twice the first in every pair. Other answers are possible: use the same positive multiplier on both terms. Ratios that describe the same comparison are called equivalent ratios. The bars showed why 1 : 2, 2 : 4, 3 : 6, 4 : 8 and 6 : 12 are equivalent. To simplify, divide both terms by a common factor. You can do this in stages: 4 : 8 = (4 ÷ 2) : (8 ÷ 2) = 2 : 4 2 : 4 = (2 ÷ 2) : (4 ÷ 2) = 1 : 2 6 : 12 = (6 ÷ 3) : (12 ÷ 3) = 2 : 4 = 1 : 2 Stop when the terms are whole numbers with no common factor greater than 1. Here that gives 1 : 2. Dividing only one term would change the comparison, rather like shrinking only one shoe in a pair. END PANEL CH03-PAGE018-PANEL01 |
