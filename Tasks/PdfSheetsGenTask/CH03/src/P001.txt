[MScene] Scene CH03 01A A safe route through the yard
[MMeta] DO NOT PRINT — Setting: Location CH03 L01 Parade Ground master. View from Food Street towards the counting arch: protected pedestrian lane follows the physical-left edge; central rehearsal loop is empty; elephant work bay is on the physical right. Costume bench is outside the loop beside the near part of the lane. Upper-right equipment gallery has a near stair and a far staff door. Warm work lamps light hands; full-moon light edges blue timber, indigo rails, brass and red-and-gold parade decoration. The guest-facing viewing rope stays separate from the working rail. A clear near-side outer route lets crew move between costume bench and work stand without cutting across the loop or the guest line.
[MMeta] Cast and positions. Trio and Tez at the Food Street threshold; Tara beside the broad helper at the pedestrian-lane entrance. The parade helper is on the elephant’s working side.
[MMeta] Props and continuity. Full-moon night continues from Chapter 02. Ahana initially carries the portable lamp right. Tara holds staff right. The float is blocked and temporarily strapped; the final strap is not fitted. First forty guests are already in Entry Court; no guest is yet in the rehearsal loop.
