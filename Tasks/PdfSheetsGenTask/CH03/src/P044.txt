END NON-PRINTING CARRIED PRODUCTION CONTEXT ORIGINAL PANEL CONTENT Practice 3C Question 3 Ameera exchanged 750 UAE Dirham and received 200 USD. How much is 1 UAE Dirham in USD? Give your answer to the nearest cent. Check your method USD means US dollars. Divide the 200 dollars by 750 to find the value of 1 dirham at this exchange rate: 200 ÷ 750 = 200/750 = 4/15 = 0.2666… USD One cent is $0.01, so we need two decimal places. In 0.2666…, the third decimal digit is 6. Round 0.26 up to 0.27. 1 UAE Dirham ≈ 0.27 USD, or 27 US cents. The ≈ sign means “approximately equal to”. Keep the unrounded value in any further calculation; 0.27 is the rounded answer requested here. END PANEL CH03-PAGE044-PANEL01 |
