(a) 0.85 m : 73 cm     (b) 1/9 : 7/9 (c) 1/4 h : 17 min     (d) 7.83 : 82.1 Check your method (a) The units differ. Since 1 m = 100 cm, change 0.85 m to centimetres first: 0.85 × 100 = 85 cm. 0.85 m : 73 cm = 85 cm : 73 cm = 85 : 73 We changed only the metre measurement because we were converting its unit. Once units match, simplifying a ratio means changing both terms together. (b) Both fractions count ninths. Multiply each by 9: (1/9 × 9) : (7/9 × 9) = (9 ÷ 9) : (63 ÷ 9) = 1 : 7 (c) One hour has 60 minutes. A quarter of an hour is 60 ÷ 4 = 15 minutes. 1/4 h : 17 min = 15 min : 17 min = 15 : 17 (d) There are two decimal places in 7.83. Multiply both terms by 100; writing 82.1 as 82.10 helps us see both changes. 7.83 : 82.10 = (7.83 × 100) : (82.10 × 100) = 783 : 8210 The whole numbers 783 and 8210 have no common factor greater than 1, so we stop there.
