[MMeta] All scene, beat, teaching-block, figure and companion IDs are non-printing production references. Word pages are review pages, not graphic-novel page assignments. Speaker names, tail targets, source attributions, locations, view descriptions and staging are non-printing. Never print a source attribution as part of a balloon. Preserve the order of captions and dialogue within each beat.
[MMeta] Each visual beat specifies one frozen image. Earlier or later actions explicitly placed between images are continuity context, not a request for multiple bodies or poses in one picture. A source caption may describe several actions across the illustrated sequence. Silent beats show necessary handovers, setup or intermediate gestures without invented dialogue.
[MMeta] The current beat’s explicit state overrides only the corresponding scene default; all other geography, possessions and equipment states persist. Later planners must copy the applicable location, scene and beat context to every receiving page and panel. Resolve named references before generating a panel. Do not copy a future solved state into an earlier question.
[MMeta] Teaching headings, explanations, examples, questions, working, answers, tables, diagrams and caption sentences are reader-facing. Supplied paragraphs styled Production Note remain non-printing. Technical V identifiers are separated from figure captions into metadata. Every question, required visual and answer remains in source order; question blanks stay blank until the solution.
[MMeta] Use exact inscriptions specified inside production notes as well as labelled speech and caption fields. Put values on their named garment tags, plans, records, arch casing or board. Do not add new signs to fill scenery. The source narration may also refer to an inscription; this is intentional, not permission to invent an extra balloon.
[MMeta] The teacher addresses the reader directly. Lessons do not move characters, consume dangerous story time or repair equipment. The reader can pause or return without changing the adventure’s clock. No magical time-freezing device, speed test or extra emergency is added.
[MMeta] Companion insets are independent character-and-scenery moments at completed teaching boundaries. Their scenery may extend into available space after the lesson is laid out, but never behind equations or across a reading path. They do not advance events or reveal a pending story answer.
[MMeta] Keep illustration identity continuous with the approved Chapter 01 and 02 character assets. Do not redesign faces, skin appearance, hair, clothing, ages or proportions while changing poses.
