Practice 3C Question 1 A fruit punch is made by mixing orange juice and apple juice in the ratio 2 : 3. Darla needs 20 L of fruit punch for a party. How much of each juice must she use? Check your method Total parts = 2 + 3 = 5;     1 part = 20 ÷ 5 = 4 L Orange juice: 2 × 4 = 8 L;     apple juice: 3 × 4 = 12 L The volumes add to 8 + 12 = 20 L. The 2 : 3 compares the two juices; orange juice is 2 of the 5 total parts, not 2 of 3 total parts. Practice 3C Question 2 Siti and Nora shared $100 in the ratio 2 : 3. How much did each girl receive? Check your method Total parts = 2 + 3 = 5;     1 part = $100 ÷ 5 = $20 Siti: 2 × $20 = $40;     Nora: 3 × $20 = $60 END PANEL CH03-PAGE043-PANEL02 |
