For its passenger capacity, 3 school children count as 2 adults. How many buses are needed for 40 adults and 40 children? We have to follow that capacity rule. Counting 80 people and treating every passenger as an adult would miss the clue about children. Let’s work it through Could 2 buses be enough? Together they have space for 2 × 30 = 60 adults. The 40 adults use 40 of those spaces, leaving 60 − 40 = 20 adult spaces. Every 2 adult spaces can take 3 children. There are 20 ÷ 2 = 10 such groups of spaces, so they can take 10 × 3 = 30 children. We have 40 children. Ten are still waiting! So 2 buses cannot carry everyone.
