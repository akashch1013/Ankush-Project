The amount 0.7 has 7 of these pieces; 0.8 has 8. Counting the same-sized pieces gives 7 : 8.  DO NOT PRINT — Figure reference V03. The following caption sentence is reader-facing. Seven tenths compared with eight tenths Production note V03 — Solution model: two equal-length wholes, each split into ten equal cells. Shade seven cells in the upper strip and eight in the lower strip. Label the rows 0.7 and 0.8 and state each cell is 0.1. Keep cell widths equal across rows. Use the current ChandRush colour and design style wherever it supports understanding. If that style would reduce clarity, retain the teaching colours, contrast and mathematical design required here. Mathematical accuracy and comprehension take priority. 0.7 : 0.8 = (0.7 × 10) : (0.8 × 10) = 7 : 8 There is no common factor greater than 1, so 7 : 8 is the simplest form. Notice that both terms were multiplied by 10. The same comparison tells us that 0.7 is 7/8 of 0.8, and 0.8 is 8/7 of 0.7. A ratio answer keeps both terms: write 7 : 8. The fraction 7/8 tells us how large the first amount is compared with the second. END PANEL CH03-PAGE009-PANEL01 |
