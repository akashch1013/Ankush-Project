Check your method Total parts = 5 + 7 = 12;     1 part = 600 ÷ 12 = 50 mL Mali: 5 × 50 = 250 mL;     her brother: 7 × 50 = 350 mL Revision Question 5 In a month, 5000 people visited an aquarium. The ratio of male visitors to female visitors was 3 : 5. How many male visitors and how many female visitors were there? Check your method Total parts = 3 + 5 = 8;     1 part = 5000 ÷ 8 = 625 visitors Male visitors: 3 × 625 = 1875 Female visitors: 5 × 625 = 3125 Check: 1875 + 3125 = 5000 visitors. Revision Question 6 A ribbon 90 cm long is cut into two pieces in the ratio 4 : 6. Find the length of the shorter piece. Check your method The shorter piece has 4 parts, because 4 is less than 6.
