More than one addition or subtraction

When an expression contains only addition and subtraction, calculate from left to right.

Take: 3 + 4 − 6

First: 3 + 4 = 7   Then: 7 − 6 = 1

Therefore: 3 + 4 − 6 = 7 − 6 = 1

+4+4+4 → 12

It may be tempting to do 4 − 6 first because those numbers are beside each other. Do not.
