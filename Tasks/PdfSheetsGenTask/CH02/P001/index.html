Ahana and Navya push the crooked cart away from Food Street’s entrance. Vihaan lifts a bowl trapped beneath its wheel.

Once the way is clear, the blue-waistcoated helper follows them in with its empty cart.

Food Street is a covered lane of counters beneath saffron and teal awnings. The cooking counter is on the left and the drinks are on the right. Near the entrance, a pale packing bench stands beside a covered doorway.
