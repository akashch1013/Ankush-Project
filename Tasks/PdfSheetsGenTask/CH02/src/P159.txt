Revision Question 6
[Question] Evaluate and give answers in simplest form.
[Question] (a)  4/5 × 2/5
[Question] (b)  2/5 × 1/7
[Question] (c)  5/9 × 31/35
[Question] (d)  1/8 × 4/15
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a)  4/5 × 2/5 = (4 × 2)/(5 × 5) = 8/25.
[WorkedStep] (b)  2/5 × 1/7 = (2 × 1)/(5 × 7) = 2/35.
[WorkedStep] (c) Divide 5 and 35 by 5 before multiplying: 5/9 × 31/35 = (1 × 31)/(9 × 7) = 31/63.
[WorkedStep] (d) Divide 4 and 8 by 4: 1/8 × 4/15 = (1 × 1)/(2 × 15) = 1/30.
Revision Question 7
[Question] Evaluate and give answers in simplest form.
[Question] (a)  4/5 ÷ 2/3
[Question] (b)  2/5 ÷ 1/3
[Question] (c)  7/9 ÷ 2/3
[Question] (d)  9/10 ÷ 4/5
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a) 4/5 ÷ 2/3 = 4/5 × 3/2. Divide 4 and 2 by 2: (2 × 3)/(5 × 1) = 6/5 = 1 1/5.
[WorkedStep] (b) 2/5 ÷ 1/3 = 2/5 × 3/1 = 6/5 = 1 1/5.
[WorkedStep] (c) 7/9 ÷ 2/3 = 7/9 × 3/2. Divide 3 and 9 by 3: (7 × 1)/(3 × 2) = 7/6 = 1 1/6.
[WorkedStep] (d) 9/10 ÷ 4/5 = 9/10 × 5/4. Divide 5 and 10 by 5: (9 × 1)/(2 × 4) = 9/8 = 1 1/8.
