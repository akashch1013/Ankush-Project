Revision Question 15
[Question] Mrs Li buys 500 g of flour at $0.28 per 100 g, one packet of sugar for $1.20, and 200 g of butter at $0.49 per 100 g. Find the total cost.
Check your method
[WorkedStep] Flour costs $0.28 for each 100 g. The 500 g of flour contains 500 ÷ 100 = 5 groups of 100 g. Flour cost = 5 × $0.28 = $1.40.
[WorkedStep] Butter costs $0.49 for each 100 g. The 200 g of butter contains 200 ÷ 100 = 2 groups of 100 g. Butter cost = 2 × $0.49 = $0.98.
[WorkedStep] The sugar packet costs $1.20. Add the three costs: $1.40 + $1.20 = $2.60; $2.60 + $0.98 = $3.58. Mrs Li pays $3.58 altogether.
Revision Question 16
[Question] A basket contains 2 kg of apples at $4.20 per kg, 3 kg of grapes at $2.50 per kg and 4 kg of bananas at $1.75 per kg.
[Question] (a)  Find the total mass.
[Question] (b)  Find the total cost.
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a)  2 + 3 + 4 = 9 kg.
[WorkedStep] (b)  Apples: 2 × $4.20 = $8.40. Grapes: 3 × $2.50 = $7.50. Bananas: 4 × $1.75 = $7.00.
[WorkedStep] Total cost: $8.40 + $7.50 + $7.00 = $22.90.
