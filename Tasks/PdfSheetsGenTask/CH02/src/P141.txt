[Question] In a Mathematics exam, 3/4 of the marks come from the Short-Answer Question Section and 1/4 from the Long-Answer Question Section. What percentage comes from each?
[Question] (a)  Short-Answer Question Section
[Question] (b)  Long-Answer Question Section
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a) Short-Answer Question Section: 3/4 × 100% = 3 × (100 ÷ 4)% = 3 × 25% = 75%.
[WorkedStep] (b) Long-Answer Question Section: 1/4 × 100% = (100 ÷ 4)% = 25%. Check: 75% + 25% = 100%, covering all the marks.
Practice 2K Question 5
[Question] A box contains red and blue balls. Of all the balls, 9/20 are red and 11/20 are blue. What percentage is each colour?
[Question] (a)  Red
[Question] (b)  Blue
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a) Red balls: 9/20 × 100% = 9 × (100 ÷ 20)% = 9 × 5% = 45%.
[WorkedStep] (b) Blue balls: 11/20 × 100% = 11 × (100 ÷ 20)% = 11 × 5% = 55%. Check: 45% + 55% = 100%.

Practice 2K Question 7
[Question] Saiful must arrange 13%, 1/8, 26%, 1/4, 0.32, 5/8 and 60% in ascending order.
[Question] (a)  How would you advise him to approach the problem?
[Question] (b)  Arrange the numbers in ascending order.
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a) Write every value as a percentage so that we compare the same kind of number. Leave 13%, 26% and 60% unchanged. Convert the fractions and the decimal as shown below. Use the original forms when writing the final order.
[WorkedStep] (b) 1/8 × 100% = (100 ÷ 8)% = 12.5%.
[WorkedStep] 1/4 × 100% = (100 ÷ 4)% = 25%.
[WorkedStep] 0.32 × 100% = 32%.
[WorkedStep] 5/8 × 100% = 5 × (100 ÷ 8)% = 5 × 12.5% = 62.5%.
[WorkedStep] Compare: 12.5% < 13% < 25% < 26% < 32% < 60% < 62.5%.
[WorkedStep] Ascending order: 1/8, 13%, 1/4, 26%, 0.32, 60%, 5/8.
