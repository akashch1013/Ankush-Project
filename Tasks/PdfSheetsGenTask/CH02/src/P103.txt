Practice 2G Question 6
[Question] Ali takes his brother to Yumz Café. The receipt lists a fruit bowl $3.50, a slice of cake $4.10, tea $2.10 and a soft drink $1.25. How much does he spend?
Check your method
[WorkedStep] Add all four prices, lining up dollars and cents: $3.50 + $4.10 = $7.60.
[WorkedStep] $7.60 + $2.10 = $9.70; $9.70 + $1.25 = $10.95. Ali spends $10.95.
Practice 2G Question 7
[Question] Jasmine buys 6 pieces of cake at $3.25 each. She estimates a total of $25.20. Explain why that estimate is not accurate and suggest a better estimate without calculating the exact total.
Check your method
[WorkedStep] One piece costs $3.25, which is less than $4. Six pieces must therefore cost less than 6 × $4 = $24. Jasmine’s estimate, $25.20, is greater than $24, so it is too high.
[WorkedStep] Try the nearby price $3.50: 6 × $3.50 = 6 × $3 + 6 × $0.50 = $18 + $3 = $21. That is a better estimate than $25.20, and we have not needed the exact bill.
Practice 2G Question 8
[Question] Bernard’s party costs $725.80 for equipment, $978.50 for food and $750 for the hall. He pays $3000. How much change should he receive?
Check your method
[WorkedStep] Total cost: $725.80 + $978.50 = $1704.30; $1704.30 + $750.00 = $2454.30.
[WorkedStep] Change: $3000.00 − $2454.30 = $545.70. Check: $2454.30 + $545.70 = $3000.00.

Practice 2G Question 9
[Question] Shop A sells notebooks for $1.55 each. Alvin buys 3.
[Question] (a)  How much does he spend?
[Question] (b)  Shop B sells 3 similar notebooks for $4.80. Which shop should you choose for 3 notebooks?
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a)  3 × $1.55 = $4.65. Alvin spends $4.65.
[WorkedStep] (b) Compare the cost of 3 notebooks at each shop: Shop A costs $4.65; Shop B costs $4.80. Since $4.65 < $4.80, choose Shop A. The saving is $4.80 − $4.65 = $0.15.
Practice 2G Question 10
[Question] A scale shows that 3 kg of fish costs $22.65. Find the cost of 1 kg, rounded to the nearest whole dollar.
Check your method
[WorkedStep] The cost of 3 kg is $22.65. To find the cost of 1 kg, split $22.65 into 3 equal amounts: $22.65 ÷ 3 = $7.55.
[WorkedStep] Round $7.55 to the nearest whole dollar. It lies between $7 and $8 and is above the halfway value, $7.50. So the rounded cost is approximately $8 per kg.
[WorkedStep] Check the cost before rounding: 3 × $7.55 = $22.65.
Practice 2G Question 11
[Question] 150 sheets of paper have a total thickness of 3.68 cm. Find the thickness of one sheet, to 3 decimal places.
Check your method
[WorkedStep] Assume all 150 sheets are equally thick. Share the total thickness, 3.68 cm, equally among 150 sheets: thickness of 1 sheet = 3.68 ÷ 150 = 0.024533… cm.
[WorkedStep] For 3 decimal places, keep 0.024. The next digit is 5, so add 0.001: 0.024 + 0.001 = 0.025. One sheet is approximately 0.025 cm thick.
