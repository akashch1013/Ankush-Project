# 2.10 Expressing a Quantity as a Percentage of Another
We have changed given fractions into percentages. Next we will build the fraction ourselves. Suppose 2 counters are selected from a group of 10 counters. The diagram shows all 10 counters; the 2 selected counters are coloured differently.
All 10 counters make the total, so put 10 underneath. The 2 selected counters go on top: 2 out of 10 is 2/10.

2 as a percentage of 10 = 2/10 × 100% = 2 × 10% = 20%.
Always finish the question “percentage of what?” In “2 as a percentage of 10”, the answer is “of 10”, so 10 goes underneath.
For example, 50% of 40 minutes means half of 40 minutes: 40 ÷ 2 = 20 minutes. But 50% of 80 marks means half of 80 marks: 80 ÷ 2 = 40 marks. The same 50% can describe different amounts because the starting amounts differ.
