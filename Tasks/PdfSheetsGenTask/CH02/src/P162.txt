Revision Question 13
[Question] Fill the three boxes in 4.2 □ 8 □ 8.8 □ 4 with +, − and × to get the greatest possible value. Use each operation exactly once.
Check your method
[WorkedStep] We must use +, − and × once each. There are 3 choices for the first box, then 2 unused operations for the second, then 1 for the last. That makes 3 × 2 × 1 = 6 possible arrangements.
[WorkedStep] Let’s check all 6. In each expression, multiply first, then do addition and subtraction from left to right. Keep the numbers 4.2, 8, 8.8 and 4 in their given positions.
[WorkedStep] 4.2 + 8 − 8.8 × 4 = 4.2 + 8 − 35.2 = 12.2 − 35.2 = −23.
[WorkedStep] 4.2 + 8 × 8.8 − 4 = 4.2 + 70.4 − 4 = 70.6.
[WorkedStep] 4.2 − 8 + 8.8 × 4 = 4.2 − 8 + 35.2 = −3.8 + 35.2 = 31.4.
[WorkedStep] 4.2 − 8 × 8.8 + 4 = 4.2 − 70.4 + 4 = −66.2 + 4 = −62.2.
[WorkedStep] 4.2 × 8 + 8.8 − 4 = 33.6 + 8.8 − 4 = 38.4.
[WorkedStep] 4.2 × 8 − 8.8 + 4 = 33.6 − 8.8 + 4 = 28.8.
[WorkedStep] The greatest value is 70.6, using +, ×, − in that order. We have checked all 6 possible arrangements.
