Example 40
[Question] Class A has 25 students and 10 receive gold awards in a fitness test. Class B has 40 students and 12 receive gold awards. Which class does better by the percentage receiving gold?
Let’s work it through
[WorkedStep] Class A has 10 gold-award students out of 25 students: 10/25 × 100% = 1000/25% = 40%.
[WorkedStep] Class B has 12 gold-award students out of 40 students: 12/40 × 100% = 1200/40% = 30%.
[WorkedStep] Since 40% > 30%, Class A does better by the percentage receiving gold. Class B has more gold awards, 12 rather than 10, but it also has more students, 40 rather than 25. That is why we compare each award count with its own class total.
Your turn after Example 40
[Question] A 100 g bag of chips contains 20 g of fibre. A 150 g bag of cereal contains 75 g of fibre. Which food contains a greater percentage of fibre?
Check your method
[WorkedStep] Chips: compare 20 g of fibre with the 100 g bag: 20/100 × 100% = 20%.
[WorkedStep] Cereal: compare 75 g of fibre with the 150 g bag: 75/150 × 100% = 1/2 × 100% = 50%.
[WorkedStep] Since 50% > 20%, cereal contains the greater percentage of fibre. Each denominator is the mass of that food’s own bag.
