Example 4
[Question] Arrange 7/5, 31/15 and 11/6 in descending order.
Let’s work it through
[WorkedStep] Write the fractions as mixed numbers: 7/5 = 1 2/5, 31/15 = 2 1/15, and 11/6 = 1 5/6.
[WorkedStep] The number 2 1/15 is greater than 2; the other two numbers are less than 2. So 31/15 comes first in descending order.
[WorkedStep] Now compare the fraction parts of 1 2/5 and 1 5/6: 2/5 and 5/6. We need a common denominator for 5 and 6.
[WorkedStep] Multiples of 5: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30. Multiples of 6: 6, 12, 18, 24, 30. The first shared multiple is 30.
[WorkedStep] 2/5 = (2 × 6)/(5 × 6) = 12/30.
[WorkedStep] 5/6 = (5 × 5)/(6 × 5) = 25/30.
[WorkedStep] Since 25/30 > 12/30, we have 1 5/6 > 1 2/5. So 11/6 comes before 7/5.
[WorkedStep] Descending order: 31/15, 11/6, 7/5.
Your turn after Example 4
[Question] Arrange 17/9, 23/10 and 14/5 in descending order.
Check your method
[WorkedStep] 17/9 = 1 8/9; 23/10 = 2 3/10; 14/5 = 2 4/5.
[WorkedStep] Compare 2 3/10 and 2 4/5. Change 4/5 to tenths: 4/5 = (4 × 2)/(5 × 2) = 8/10.
[WorkedStep] Since 8/10 > 3/10, the order is 2 8/10 > 2 3/10 > 1 8/9.
[WorkedStep] Descending order in the original fractions: 14/5, 23/10, 17/9.
