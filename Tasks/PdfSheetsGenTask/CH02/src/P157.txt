Revision Question 2
[Question] Arrange 5/3, 10/7 and 2 in ascending order.
Check your method
[WorkedStep] 5/3 = 1 2/3 and 10/7 = 1 3/7; both are less than 2.
[WorkedStep] Compare 2/3 = (2 × 7)/(3 × 7) = 14/21 and 3/7 = (3 × 3)/(7 × 3) = 9/21. Since 9/21 < 14/21, ascending order is 10/7, 5/3, 2.
Revision Question 3
[Question] Arrange 12/5, 17/10 and 2 1/10 in descending order.
Check your method
[WorkedStep] Write 12/5 = 2 2/5. Change its fraction part to tenths: 2/5 = (2 × 2)/(5 × 2) = 4/10, so 12/5 = 2 4/10. Also, 17/10 = 1 7/10.
[WorkedStep] Compare 2 4/10 > 2 1/10 > 1 7/10. Descending order: 12/5, 2 1/10, 17/10.
Revision Question 4
[Question] Evaluate and give answers in simplest form.
[Question] (a)  2 1/3 + 1 2/3
[Question] (b)  1 4/5 + 3 3/5
[Question] (c)  2 1/6 + 1 5/12
[Question] (d)  3 1/7 + 1 5/14
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a) 2 1/3 + 1 2/3 = 3 + (1 + 2)/3 = 3 + 1 = 4.
[WorkedStep] (b) 1 4/5 + 3 3/5 = 4 + 7/5 = 4 + 1 2/5 = 5 2/5.
[WorkedStep] (c) 1/6 = (1 × 2)/(6 × 2) = 2/12. So 2 1/6 + 1 5/12 = 3 + (2 + 5)/12 = 3 7/12.
[WorkedStep] (d) 1/7 = (1 × 2)/(7 × 2) = 2/14. So 3 1/7 + 1 5/14 = 4 + 7/14. Simplify 7/14 = (7 ÷ 7)/(14 ÷ 7) = 1/2, giving 4 1/2.
