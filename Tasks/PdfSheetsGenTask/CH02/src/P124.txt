[PanelizationLabel] ORIGINAL PANEL CONTENT
Let’s make “out of 100” visible. One square grid is one whole. It has 10 rows of 10 equal squares, so 10 × 10 = 100 small squares. Each small square is 1/100 of the whole: we write that as 1% and say “one percent”.
For example, 8% means 8 of these hundredths: 8% = 8/100 = (8 ÷ 4)/(100 ÷ 4) = 2/25. Percentages can describe marks, attendance or part of an amount. The whole is whatever group or amount we are comparing with.
