## Practice 2J
Practice 2J Question 1
[Question] Express each percentage as a fraction in simplest form.
[Question] (a)  5%
[Question] (b)  32%
[Question] (c)  112%
[Question] (d)  150%
[Question] (e)  2 1/2%
[Question] (f)  12.5%
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a)  5% = 5/100 = (5 ÷ 5)/(100 ÷ 5) = 1/20.
[WorkedStep] (b)  32% = 32/100 = (32 ÷ 4)/(100 ÷ 4) = 8/25.
[WorkedStep] (c)  112% = 112/100 = (112 ÷ 4)/(100 ÷ 4) = 28/25. This is 28/25 = 1 3/25.
[WorkedStep] (d)  150% = 150/100 = (150 ÷ 50)/(100 ÷ 50) = 3/2. This is 3/2 = 1 1/2.
[WorkedStep] (e)  2 1/2 = (2 × 2 + 1)/2 = 5/2. Now apply the percent sign: divide this number by 100.
[WorkedStep] 2 1/2% = 5/2 × 1/100 = 5/200 = (5 ÷ 5)/(200 ÷ 5) = 1/40.
[WorkedStep] (f) 12.5% = 12.5/100. Multiply top and bottom by 10: 12.5/100 = (12.5 × 10)/(100 × 10) = 125/1000.
[WorkedStep] Simplify: 125/1000 = (125 ÷ 125)/(1000 ÷ 125) = 1/8.
Practice 2J Question 2
[Question] Express each percentage as a decimal.
[Question] (a)  68%
[Question] (b)  95%
[Question] (c)  199%
[Question] (d)  6 1/2%
[Question] (e)  40 1/2%
[Question] (f)  50.7%
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a)  68% = 68 ÷ 100 = 0.68.
[WorkedStep] (b)  95% = 95 ÷ 100 = 0.95.
[WorkedStep] (c) 199% = 199 ÷ 100 = 1.99. Since 199% is more than 100%, its decimal is more than 1.
[WorkedStep] (d) 6 1/2 = 6.5, so 6 1/2% = 6.5 ÷ 100 = 0.065.
[WorkedStep] (e) 40 1/2 = 40.5, so 40 1/2% = 40.5 ÷ 100 = 0.405.
[WorkedStep] (f)  50.7% = 50.7 ÷ 100 = 0.507.
