Revision Question 17
[Question] Express each percentage as a fraction in simplest form.
[Question] (a)  130%
[Question] (b)  25%
[Question] (c)  24.2%
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a)  130% = 130/100 = (130 ÷ 10)/(100 ÷ 10) = 13/10. This is 13/10 = 1 3/10.
[WorkedStep] (b)  25% = 25/100 = (25 ÷ 25)/(100 ÷ 25) = 1/4.
[WorkedStep] (c)  24.2% = 24.2/100 = (24.2 × 10)/(100 × 10) = 242/1000.
[WorkedStep] Divide both by 2: 242/1000 = (242 ÷ 2)/(1000 ÷ 2) = 121/500.
Revision Question 18
[Question] Convert each percentage to a decimal.
[Question] (a)  11%
[Question] (b)  205%
[Question] (c)  13.6%
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a)  11% = 11 ÷ 100 = 0.11.
[WorkedStep] (b)  205% = 205 ÷ 100 = 2.05.
[WorkedStep] (c)  13.6% = 13.6 ÷ 100 = 0.136.
Revision Question 19
[Question] Express each as a percentage.
[Question] (a)  3/5
[Question] (b)  2.56
[Question] (c)  0.15
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a) 3/5 × 100% = 3 × (100 ÷ 5)% = 3 × 20% = 60%.
[WorkedStep] Keep the % sign: 3/5 equals 60%, not 60.
[WorkedStep] (b)  2.56 × 100% = 256%.
[WorkedStep] (c)  0.15 × 100% = 15%.
