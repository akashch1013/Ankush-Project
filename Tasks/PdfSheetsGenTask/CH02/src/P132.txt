[WorkedStep] (a) 25% = 25/100 = 1/4. Add 1/4 of the usual loaf to 1 usual loaf: 1 + 1/4 = 1 1/4. Diagram II shows that total, so match 25% more to II.
[WorkedStep] (b) 50% = 50/100 = 1/2. Total: 1 + 1/2 = 1 1/2 times the usual loaf. Match I.
[WorkedStep] (c) 150% = 150/100 = 1 1/2. This is the extra amount. Total: 1 + 1 1/2 = 2 1/2 times the usual loaf. Match IV.
[WorkedStep] (d) 200% = 200/100 = 2. This adds 2 usual loaves to the original 1: 1 + 2 = 3. Match III.
[WorkedStep] In every diagram, gold shows the original loaf and teal shows the extra bread. For 150% more, for example, compare the teal length with 1 gold loaf; then include the gold loaf to get the total.
