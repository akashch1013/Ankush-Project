Check your method
Figure 3 has 8 complete shaded rows, each containing 10 squares. That is 80 shaded squares out of 100: 80% = 80/100 = (80 ÷ 20)/(100 ÷ 20) = 4/5.
In Figure 4, the first grid has 100 shaded squares and the second has 40. Count them together: 100 + 40 = 140 shaded squares. One whole is still 1 grid of 100 squares, so the shaded amount is 140/100 = 140%.
Simplify: 140/100 = (140 ÷ 20)/(100 ÷ 20) = 7/5 = 1 2/5. Here 140% is greater than 100% because the shaded amount is greater than 1 full grid.
To change a percentage into a fraction, write its number over 100, then simplify. For example, 20% means 20/100; it does not mean 20 whole grids. Let’s work through that conversion.

Example 29
[Question] Express each percentage as a fraction in simplest form.
[Question] (a)  20%
[Question] (b)  150%
Let’s work it through
[WorkedStep] (a) 20% means 20/100. Divide both numbers by 20: 20/100 = (20 ÷ 20)/(100 ÷ 20) = 1/5.
[WorkedStep] (b) 150% = 150/100 = (150 ÷ 50)/(100 ÷ 50) = 3/2 = 1 1/2. That fits the picture we used: 100% gives 1 whole, and the extra 50% gives half a whole.
