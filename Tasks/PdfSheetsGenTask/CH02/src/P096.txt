For 21.9 ÷ 3, we will share 21 wholes and 9 tenths among 3 equal groups. Keeping the whole and decimal parts visible makes this division easier to follow.
Example 23
[Question] Estimate without a calculator. Then evaluate.
[Question] (a)  21.9 ÷ 3
[Question] (b)  50.8 ÷ 20
Let’s work it through
[WorkedStep] (a)  Choose a nearby multiple of 3: 21 ÷ 3 = 7 is a useful estimate.
[WorkedStep] Share the 21 wholes: 21 ÷ 3 = 7 wholes per group.
[WorkedStep] Share the 9 tenths: 9 ÷ 3 = 3 tenths per group, or 0.3.
[WorkedStep] Add the two parts of each share: 7 + 0.3 = 7.3. So 21.9 ÷ 3 = 7.3. Check: 7.3 × 3 = 21.9.
[WorkedStep] (b)  Estimate: 50 ÷ 20 = 2.5.
[WorkedStep] Since 20 = 10 × 2, we can divide by 10, then by 2: 50.8 ÷ 20 = (50.8 ÷ 10) ÷ 2.
[WorkedStep] First 50.8 ÷ 10 = 5.08. Now divide 5.08 by 2.
[WorkedStep] The 5 ones give 2 ones to each share, leaving 5 − 4 = 1 one. Change that remaining one to 10 tenths. Since 5.08 has 0 tenths, there are 10 tenths to share: 10 ÷ 2 = 5 tenths per share.
[WorkedStep] Finally, share the 8 hundredths: 8 ÷ 2 = 4 hundredths per share.
[WorkedStep] Each share is 2 + 0.5 + 0.04 = 2.54. Therefore 50.8 ÷ 20 = 2.54. Check: 2.54 × 20 = 50.8.
Your turn after Example 23
[Question] Estimate without a calculator. Then evaluate.
[Question] (a)  30.2 ÷ 2
[Question] (b)  108.6 ÷ 60
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a) Estimate: 30 ÷ 2 = 15. Share the whole and fraction parts: 30 ÷ 2 = 15 and 0.2 ÷ 2 = 0.1. So 30.2 ÷ 2 = 15 + 0.1 = 15.1.
[WorkedStep] (b) Estimate 108.6 ÷ 60 with 108 ÷ 60 = 1.8. Since 60 = 6 × 10, divide by 6, then by 10.
[WorkedStep] 108.6 ÷ 6 = 18.1, because 108 ÷ 6 = 18 and 0.6 ÷ 6 = 0.1. Then 18.1 ÷ 10 = 1.81. Check: 1.81 × 60 = 108.6.
