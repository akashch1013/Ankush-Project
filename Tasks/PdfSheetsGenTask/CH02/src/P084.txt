Example 19
[Question] Use a calculator and round as requested.
[Question] (a)  √2 to 3 decimal places
[Question] (b)  √5 to 4 decimal places
[Question] (c)  ∛9 to 5 decimal places
Let’s work it through
[WorkedStep] (a) The calculator gives √2 = 1.414213… . For 3 decimal places, keep 1.414. The next digit is 2, below 5, so leave 1.414 unchanged. Answer: √2 ≈ 1.414.
[WorkedStep] (b) The calculator gives √5 = 2.236067… . For 4 decimal places, keep 2.2360. The next digit is 6, so add 0.0001: 2.2360 + 0.0001 = 2.2361. Answer: √5 ≈ 2.2361.
[WorkedStep] (c) ∛9 = 2.080083… . Keep 2.08008 for 5 decimal places; the next digit is 3. So ∛9 ≈ 2.08008.
Ali asks how to give √4 and ∛8 to two decimal places. Both equal 2 exactly: 2 × 2 = 4 and 2 × 2 × 2 = 8. Write 2.00 for each when two decimal places are requested. The zeros show the requested format; they do not change the value.
