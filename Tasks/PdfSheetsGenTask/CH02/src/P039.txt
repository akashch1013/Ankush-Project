# 2.3 Multiplication and Division of Proper Fractions
What does 1/3 × 5 mean? It means 5 lots of 1/3.
In this picture, each small blue rectangle is 1/3 of a whole bar. Count 5 of them. Three pieces fill 1 whole bar; 2 pieces remain. So we have 1 whole and 2/3 of another whole.

1/3 × 5 = 1/3 + 1/3 + 1/3 + 1/3 + 1/3 = 5/3 = 1 2/3.
Now we will find 1/5 of 1/3. This means taking only one fifth of the shaded third, rather than taking 5 copies of it. Let’s show the difference with a picture.
