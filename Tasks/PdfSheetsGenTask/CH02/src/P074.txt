## Practice 2E
Practice 2E Question 1
[Question] Fill each box with <, > or =.
[Question] (a)  8.3 □ 83/100
[Question] (b)  9/5 □ 1.8
[Question] (c)  1 1/10 □ 0.67
[Question] (d)  3.25 □ 13/4
[Question] (e)  4.13 □ 25/4
[Question] (f)  66/20 □ 3.1
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a)  83/100 = 0.83. Compare whole parts: 8 > 0. Therefore 8.3 > 83/100.
[WorkedStep] (b)  9/5 = (9 × 2)/(5 × 2) = 18/10 = 1.8, so the symbol is =.
[WorkedStep] (c)  1 1/10 = 1.1, which is greater than 0.67. The symbol is >.
[WorkedStep] (d)  13/4 = (13 × 25)/(4 × 25) = 325/100 = 3.25, so the symbol is =.
[WorkedStep] (e)  25/4 = (25 × 25)/(4 × 25) = 625/100 = 6.25. Since 4.13 < 6.25, the symbol is <.
[WorkedStep] (f)  66/20 = (66 ÷ 2)/(20 ÷ 2) = 33/10 = 3.3. Since 3.3 > 3.1, the symbol is >.
Practice 2E Question 2
[Question] Arrange the numbers in ascending order.
[Question] (a)  0.005, 5/10, 50/1000
[Question] (b)  6.05, 605/1000, 6 5/10
[Question] (c)  1.4, 4.54, 54/1000
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a) Write 5/10 = 0.5 and 50/1000 = 0.050 = 0.05. Compare all three using 3 decimal places: 0.005 < 0.050 < 0.500.
[WorkedStep] Ascending order: 0.005, 50/1000, 5/10.
[WorkedStep] (b) 605/1000 = 0.605 and 6 5/10 = 6.5. Compare: 0.605 < 6.05 < 6.50.
[WorkedStep] Ascending order: 605/1000, 6.05, 6 5/10.
[WorkedStep] (c) Write 54/1000 = 0.054. The whole-number parts of 0.054, 1.4 and 4.54 are 0, 1 and 4. So 0.054 < 1.4 < 4.54.
[WorkedStep] Ascending order: 54/1000, 1.4, 4.54.
