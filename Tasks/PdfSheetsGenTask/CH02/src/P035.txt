Practice 2B Question 3
[Question] Evaluate. Give answers in simplest form.
[Question] (a)  2 3/5 + 1 3/10
[Question] (b)  3 7/10 + 1 1/2
[Question] (c)  2 1/3 + 3 5/9
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a) 3/5 = (3 × 2)/(5 × 2) = 6/10.
[WorkedStep] 2 3/5 + 1 3/10 = 3 + (6 + 3)/10 = 3 9/10.
[WorkedStep] (b) 1/2 = (1 × 5)/(2 × 5) = 5/10.
[WorkedStep] 3 7/10 + 1 1/2 = 4 + (7 + 5)/10 = 4 + 12/10.
[WorkedStep] Since 12/10 = 1 2/10 = 1 1/5, the total is 5 1/5.
[WorkedStep] (c) 1/3 = (1 × 3)/(3 × 3) = 3/9.
[WorkedStep] 2 1/3 + 3 5/9 = 5 + (3 + 5)/9 = 5 8/9.
Practice 2B Question 4
[Question] Estimate first, then find the actual value. Give answers in simplest form.
[Question] (a)  3 1/2 + 1 2/3
[Question] (b)  10 5/7 + 8 5/14
[Question] (c)  9 5/9 + 7 1/3
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a)  Estimate: 3 + 2 = 5.
[WorkedStep] Use denominator 6: 1/2 = (1 × 3)/(2 × 3) = 3/6; 2/3 = (2 × 2)/(3 × 2) = 4/6.
[WorkedStep] 3 1/2 + 1 2/3 = 4 + 3/6 + 4/6 = 4 + 7/6.
[WorkedStep] Regroup 7/6 = 1 1/6, giving 4 + 1 1/6 = 5 1/6.
[WorkedStep] (b)  Estimate: 11 + 8 = 19.
[WorkedStep] Change 5/7 to (5 × 2)/(7 × 2) = 10/14.
[WorkedStep] 10 5/7 + 8 5/14 = 18 + 10/14 + 5/14 = 18 + 15/14.
[WorkedStep] Regroup 15/14 = 1 1/14, giving 18 + 1 1/14 = 19 1/14.
[WorkedStep] (c)  Estimate: 10 + 7 = 17.
[WorkedStep] Change 1/3 to (1 × 3)/(3 × 3) = 3/9.
[WorkedStep] 9 5/9 + 7 1/3 = 16 + (5 + 3)/9 = 16 8/9.
