[PanelizationLabel] ORIGINAL PANEL CONTENT
What about 7/10 + 1/5? The denominators 10 and 5 are different. Let’s give both fractions denominator 10 before adding.
Each full rectangle in the next picture is 1 whole. Split each fifth into 2 equal parts. That makes 5 × 2 = 10 parts in the whole, and the shaded fifth becomes 1 × 2 = 2 tenths:
1/5 = (1 × 2)/(5 × 2) = 2/10.

[PanelizationLabel] ORIGINAL PANEL CONTENT
For 1/2 − 1/6, use denominator 6: 1/2 = (1 × 3)/(2 × 3) = 3/6.
Now subtract the top numbers and keep denominator 6:
1/2 − 1/6 = 3/6 − 1/6 = 2/6 = (2 ÷ 2)/(6 ÷ 2) = 1/3.
Matching denominators lets us add or subtract equal-sized pieces. The denominator tells us the piece size; it stays the same.
