Practice 2B Question 5
[Question] Read each method. Comment on it and improve the answer.
[Question] (a)  James writes: 1 2/3 + 2 5/6 = 5/3 + 17/6 = 10/6 + 17/6 = 27/6.
[Question] (b)  Serene writes: 1 2/3 + 2 5/6 = 1 4/6 + 2 5/6 = 3 9/6 = 3 3/2.
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a) James’s method is correct. He changes both mixed numbers to improper fractions: 1 2/3 = (1 × 3 + 2)/3 = 5/3, and 2 5/6 = (2 × 6 + 5)/6 = 17/6.
[WorkedStep] He then uses 5/3 = (5 × 2)/(3 × 2) = 10/6 and adds 10/6 + 17/6 = 27/6.
[WorkedStep] His answer can be simplified: 27/6 = (27 ÷ 3)/(6 ÷ 3) = 9/2 = 4 1/2.
[WorkedStep] (b) Serene’s calculation gives the right amount too. But in her answer, 3 3/2, the fraction part 3/2 is larger than 1. A mixed number should have a fraction part smaller than 1.
[WorkedStep] Change 3/2 to 1 1/2, then add: 3 + 1 1/2 = 4 1/2.
[WorkedStep] Both methods give 4 1/2.
