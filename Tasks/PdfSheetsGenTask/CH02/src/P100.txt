## Practice 2G
Some questions below contain brackets. Calculate what is inside each pair of brackets first. For example, in (4 + 2) − 3, first find 4 + 2 = 6; then find 6 − 3 = 3.
A calculation such as 3.8 − 8.51 goes below zero because we subtract more than we started with. We will show the steps when we reach that question, then practise negative decimals in the next section.
Practice 2G Question 1
[Question] Estimate without a calculator, then evaluate.
[Question] (a)  20.1 + 2.1
[Question] (b)  77.8 + 3.2
[Question] (c)  16.4 − 5.5
[Question] (d)  66.02 − 12.11
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a)  Estimate: 20 + 2 = 22. Exact: 20.1 + 2.1 = 22.2.
[WorkedStep] (b)  Estimate: 78 + 3 = 81. Exact: 77.8 + 3.2 = 81.0 = 81.
[WorkedStep] (c) Estimate: 16 − 6 = 10. For the exact answer, line up 16.4 and 5.5, as shown on the left.
[WorkedStep] (d) Estimate: 66 − 12 = 54. Line up 66.02 and 12.11, as shown on the right. The small numbers record the borrowing.
