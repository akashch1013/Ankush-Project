Example 6
[Question] Evaluate each sum.
[Question] (a)  2 1/3 + 1 1/6
[Question] (b)  3 1/5 + 1 3/10
Let’s work it through
[WorkedStep] (a) We are adding 2 1/3 + 1 1/6. For a quick estimate, use 2 instead of 2 1/3 and 1 instead of 1 1/6: 2 + 1 = 3. The exact answer will be a little higher.
[WorkedStep] Add the whole-number parts: 2 + 1 = 3.
[WorkedStep] Next add 1/3 and 1/6. Their denominators differ, so make them match:
[WorkedStep] 1/3 = (1 × 2)/(3 × 2) = 2/6. Keep the other fraction, 1/6, unchanged.
[WorkedStep] Add the fractions: 2/6 + 1/6 = 3/6. Together with the 3 wholes, that gives 3 3/6.
[WorkedStep] Simplify: 3/6 = (3 ÷ 3)/(6 ÷ 3) = 1/2.
[WorkedStep] Answer: 3 1/2. Check: 3 1/2 is 1/2 more than our estimate of 3.
[WorkedStep] (b) For 3 1/5 + 1 3/10, an estimate using nearby halves is 3 + 1 1/2 = 4 1/2.
[WorkedStep] For the exact sum, add the whole-number parts: 3 + 1 = 4. Then give the fraction parts, 1/5 and 3/10, denominator 10:
[WorkedStep] 1/5 = (1 × 2)/(5 × 2) = 2/10. The fraction 3/10 stays as it is.
[WorkedStep] Add: 2/10 + 3/10 = 5/10. Simplify: 5/10 = (5 ÷ 5)/(10 ÷ 5) = 1/2.
[WorkedStep] So 3 1/5 + 1 3/10 = 4 + 1/2 = 4 1/2. Our estimate, 4 1/2, happens to equal the exact answer this time.
