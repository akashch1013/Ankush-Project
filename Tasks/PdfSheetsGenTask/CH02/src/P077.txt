Practice 2E Question 8
[Question] Use the fractions 3/4, 1/5, 8/10, 9/20 and 11/5.
[Question] (a)  Express each as a decimal, then arrange the fractions in ascending order.
[Question] (b)  Without converting to decimals, explain how to arrange the fractions in descending order.
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a)  3/4 = (3 × 25)/(4 × 25) = 75/100 = 0.75; 1/5 = (1 × 2)/(5 × 2) = 2/10 = 0.2; 8/10 = 0.8; 9/20 = (9 × 5)/(20 × 5) = 45/100 = 0.45; 11/5 = (11 × 2)/(5 × 2) = 22/10 = 2.2.
[WorkedStep] Compare 0.2 < 0.45 < 0.75 < 0.8 < 2.2. Ascending order: 1/5, 9/20, 3/4, 8/10, 11/5.
[WorkedStep] (b)  11/5 is the only fraction greater than 1, so it comes first. Use twentieths for the others: 3/4 = (3 × 5)/(4 × 5) = 15/20; 1/5 = (1 × 4)/(5 × 4) = 4/20; 8/10 = (8 × 2)/(10 × 2) = 16/20; 9/20 stays 9/20.
[WorkedStep] Compare 16 > 15 > 9 > 4. Descending order: 11/5, 8/10, 3/4, 9/20, 1/5.
