## Converting Decimals to Fractions
Now let’s change a decimal back to a fraction. Read the place of its last digit. In 0.6, the 6 is in the tenths place, so 0.6 = 6/10. In 0.35, the 5 is in the hundredths place, so 0.35 = 35/100.
Count only the digits after the decimal point: 1 place means denominator 10, 2 places mean 100, and 3 places mean 1000. Then simplify the fraction if possible.
Example 12
[Question] Convert these decimals to fractions in simplest form.
[Question] (a)  0.6
[Question] (b)  1.35
Let’s work it through
[WorkedStep] (a)  0.6 is six tenths, so 0.6 = 6/10. Divide top and bottom by 2: 6/10 = (6 ÷ 2)/(10 ÷ 2) = 3/5.
[WorkedStep] Check: 3 ÷ 5 = 0.6.
[WorkedStep] (b) In 1.35, the 1 is a whole and the .35 means 35 hundredths: 1.35 = 1 + 35/100.
[WorkedStep] Simplify the fraction part: 35/100 = (35 ÷ 5)/(100 ÷ 5) = 7/20.
[WorkedStep] So 1.35 = 1 7/20.
[WorkedStep] We can also write an improper fraction. The 1 whole is 100/100, so 1.35 = 100/100 + 35/100 = 135/100.
[WorkedStep] Divide top and bottom by 5: 135/100 = (135 ÷ 5)/(100 ÷ 5) = 27/20. Both 1 7/20 and 27/20 are correct.
