Example 25
[Question] Calculate.
[Question] (a)  6.8 × 10²
[Question] (b)  561 × 10⁴
[Question] (c)  127.4 ÷ 10³
[Question] (d)  53 285 ÷ 10⁴
Let’s work it through
[WorkedStep] (a) 10² = 100, so multiply by 10 twice: 6.8 → 68 → 680. Therefore 6.8 × 10² = 680.
[WorkedStep] (b) 10⁴ means four factors of 10. Multiply by 10 at each arrow: 561 → 5610 → 56 100 → 561 000 → 5 610 000. So 561 × 10⁴ = 5 610 000.
[WorkedStep] (c) Since 10³ = 1000, divide by 10 three times: 127.4 ÷ 10 = 12.74; 12.74 ÷ 10 = 1.274; 1.274 ÷ 10 = 0.1274. So 127.4 ÷ 1000 = 0.1274.
[WorkedStep] (d) Since 10⁴ = 10 000, divide by 10 four times: 53 285 → 5328.5 → 532.85 → 53.285 → 5.3285. Each arrow means ÷ 10. So 53 285 ÷ 10 000 = 5.3285.
