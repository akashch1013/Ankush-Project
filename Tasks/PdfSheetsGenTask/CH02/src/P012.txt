[TeacherBridge] Before we choose a label, let’s be clear about the whole we are measuring. We’ll begin with equal shares, then compare fractions and mixed numbers.

Imagine one pie on a plate and four people ready to share it. Would one tiny piece and three large pieces be fair? Let’s cut the pie into 4 equal pieces instead.
The circle in this picture represents 1 whole pie. Each of its 4 equal pieces is one quarter of the pie. We write one quarter as 1/4.
