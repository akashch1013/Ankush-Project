Your turn after Example 12
[Question] Convert these decimals to fractions in simplest form.
[Question] (a)  0.42
[Question] (b)  12.5
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a) The decimal 0.42 has 2 digits after the point, so it means 42 hundredths: 0.42 = 42/100 = (42 ÷ 2)/(100 ÷ 2) = 21/50.
[WorkedStep] (b) Keep the 12 wholes in 12.5. The fraction part is 5/10 = (5 ÷ 5)/(10 ÷ 5) = 1/2. So 12.5 = 12 1/2.
[WorkedStep] As an improper fraction, 12 1/2 = (12 × 2 + 1)/2 = 25/2.
[WorkedStep] A calculator can check a fraction-to-decimal conversion. For 3/4, enter 3 ÷ 4, then press equals. The answer should be 0.75.
[WorkedStep] Some scientific calculators also have a key that switches between a fraction and its decimal. Its label and position depend on the calculator. The picture shows the idea: 3/4 and 0.75 are two ways to write the same number.
