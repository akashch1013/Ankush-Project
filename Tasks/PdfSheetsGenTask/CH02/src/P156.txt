# Chapter 2 Revision
Try each question before reading its worked answer. If you get stuck, ask what you need to do: compare numbers, add amounts, change their form or find a percentage. Then choose the method that fits.
Revision Question 1
[Question] Circle the greater fraction in each pair.
[Question] (a)  5/4, 7/6
[Question] (b)  6/5, 13/10
[Question] (c)  8/7, 27/20
[Question] (d)  12/13, 20/17
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a)  Use denominator 12 for both fractions: 5/4 = (5 × 3)/(4 × 3) = 15/12, 7/6 = (7 × 2)/(6 × 2) = 14/12.
[WorkedStep] Since 15/12 > 14/12, circle 5/4, the greater fraction.
[WorkedStep] (b)  Use denominator 10 for both fractions: 6/5 = (6 × 2)/(5 × 2) = 12/10.
[WorkedStep] Since 12/10 < 13/10, circle 13/10, the greater fraction.
[WorkedStep] (c) Use denominator 140: 8/7 = (8 × 20)/(7 × 20) = 160/140, 27/20 = (27 × 7)/(20 × 7) = 189/140.
[WorkedStep] Since 160/140 < 189/140, circle 27/20, the greater fraction.
[WorkedStep] (d) Compare each fraction with 1. Since 12 < 13, 12/13 < 1. Since 20 > 17, 20/17 > 1.
[WorkedStep] So 12/13 < 1 < 20/17. Circle 20/17. No common denominator is needed here.
