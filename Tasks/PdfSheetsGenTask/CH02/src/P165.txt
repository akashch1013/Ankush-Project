[PanelizationLabel] ORIGINAL PANEL CONTENT
We have found percentages such as 5 as a percentage of 10: 5/10 × 100% = 50%. In the next question, the percentage is given and we must find the amount.
Start with 1%. It means 1/100, so divide the full amount by 100. For example, 1% of 80 = 80 ÷ 100 = 0.8. To find 10%, take 10 of those 1% shares: 10 × 0.8 = 8.
We can also write 10% of 80 = 10/100 × 80 = 8. The word “of” asks us to take that fraction of the given amount. Let’s use this method with 5000 and then 600 mL.
Revision Question 20
[Question] Evaluate.
[Question] (a)  30% of 5000
[Question] (b)  45% of 600 mL
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a)  30% means 30 out of 100 equal shares. One share of 5000 is 5000 ÷ 100 = 50. Take 30 shares: 50 × 30 = 1500.
[WorkedStep] So 30% of 5000 = 1500. Check: 1500/5000 = 3/10 = 30%.
[WorkedStep] (b) First find 1% of 600 mL: 600 ÷ 100 = 6 mL. Then take 45 of those shares: 45 × 6 mL = 270 mL.
[WorkedStep] Check: 50% would be 300 mL. Five percent is 5 × 6 = 30 mL, so 45% should be 300 − 30 = 270 mL. It is.
