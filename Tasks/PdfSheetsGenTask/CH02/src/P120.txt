Practice 2I Question 3
[Question] Lisa weighs 45.5 kg. Mei weighs 2.1 kg more than Lisa, and Nurul weighs 1.6 kg more than Mei.
[Question] (a)  Find Mei’s weight.
[Question] (b)  Find Nurul’s weight.
[Question] (c)  The machine is later found to read 1.2 kg more than each person’s actual weight. Find the actual weight of each girl.
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a) Lisa’s reading is 45.5 kg. Mei’s reading is 2.1 kg more: 45.5 + 2.1 = 47.6 kg.
[WorkedStep] (b) Mei’s reading is 47.6 kg. Nurul’s reading is 1.6 kg more: 47.6 + 1.6 = 49.2 kg.
[WorkedStep] (c) Every reading is 1.2 kg too high. Subtract 1.2 kg from each reading to find each actual weight.
[WorkedStep] Lisa: 45.5 − 1.2 = 44.3 kg. Mei: 47.6 − 1.2 = 46.4 kg. Nurul: 49.2 − 1.2 = 48.0 kg.
[WorkedStep] Check the differences: 46.4 − 44.3 = 2.1 kg, and 48.0 − 46.4 = 1.6 kg. The differences stay the same because we subtract 1.2 kg from all three readings.
Practice 2I Question 4
[Question] A shopkeeper sells 3 bottles of detergent at $6.80 each. The customer pays $30.
[Question] (a)  Find the total cost.
[Question] (b)  Find the change.
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a)  3 × $6.80 = $20.40.
[WorkedStep] (b)  $30.00 − $20.40 = $9.60. Check: $20.40 + $9.60 = $30.00.
Practice 2I Question 5
[Question] Fish costs $12.30 per kg and clams cost $15.50 per kg. Maria buys 2 kg of fish and 3 kg of clams.
[Question] (a)  How much must she pay?
[Question] (b)  She pays with $100. How much change does she receive?
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a)  Fish: 2 × $12.30 = $24.60. Clams: 3 × $15.50 = $46.50. Total: $24.60 + $46.50 = $71.10.
[WorkedStep] (b)  $100.00 − $71.10 = $28.90. Check: $71.10 + $28.90 = $100.00.
