[Question] Arrange the fractions in ascending order.
[Question] (a)  3/2, 6/5, 13/10
[Question] (b)  7/4, 23/12, 11/6
[Question] (c)  45/19, 89/38, 7/2
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a)  Use a common denominator of 10: 3/2 = (3 × 5)/(2 × 5) = 15/10, 6/5 = (6 × 2)/(5 × 2) = 12/10, 13/10.
[WorkedStep] Smallest to greatest: 6/5, 13/10, 3/2.
[WorkedStep] (b)  Use a common denominator of 12: 7/4 = (7 × 3)/(4 × 3) = 21/12, 23/12, 11/6 = (11 × 2)/(6 × 2) = 22/12.
[WorkedStep] Smallest to greatest: 7/4, 11/6, 23/12.
[WorkedStep] (c)  Use a common denominator of 38: 45/19 = (45 × 2)/(19 × 2) = 90/38, 89/38, 7/2 = (7 × 19)/(2 × 19) = 133/38.
[WorkedStep] Smallest to greatest: 89/38, 45/19, 7/2.

[Question] Arrange the fractions in descending order.
[Question] (a)  19/14, 10/7, 3/2
[Question] (b)  32/13, 61/26, 31/13
[Question] (c)  39/11, 7/2, 81/22
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a)  Use a common denominator of 14: 19/14, 10/7 = (10 × 2)/(7 × 2) = 20/14, 3/2 = (3 × 7)/(2 × 7) = 21/14.
[WorkedStep] Greatest to smallest: 3/2, 10/7, 19/14.
[WorkedStep] (b)  Use a common denominator of 26: 32/13 = (32 × 2)/(13 × 2) = 64/26, 61/26, 31/13 = (31 × 2)/(13 × 2) = 62/26.
[WorkedStep] Greatest to smallest: 32/13, 31/13, 61/26.
[WorkedStep] (c)  Use a common denominator of 22: 39/11 = (39 × 2)/(11 × 2) = 78/22, 7/2 = (7 × 11)/(2 × 11) = 77/22, 81/22.
[WorkedStep] Greatest to smallest: 81/22, 39/11, 7/2.
