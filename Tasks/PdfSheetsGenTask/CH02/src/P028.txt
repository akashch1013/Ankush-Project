# 2.2 Addition of Mixed Numbers
So far, we have compared fractions. Now let’s add them. Start with 1/6 + 2/6.
Each full rectangle in the next picture represents 1 whole, cut into 6 equal parts. One blue part shows 1/6. Two gold parts show 2/6.
Put the coloured parts together. We have 1 + 2 = 3 parts, and each part is still one sixth, 1/6, of the whole. So the total is 3/6.
