[Question] Evaluate and give answers in simplest form.
[Question] (a)  1/2 ÷ 1/3
[Question] (b)  4/5 ÷ 2/3
[Question] (c)  3/5 ÷ 4/5
[Question] (d)  4/9 ÷ 5/3
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a) 1/2 ÷ 1/3 = 1/2 × 3/1 = 3/2 = 1 1/2.
[WorkedStep] (b) 4/5 ÷ 2/3 = 4/5 × 3/2 = ((4 ÷ 2) × 3)/(5 × (2 ÷ 2)) = 6/5 = 1 1/5.
[WorkedStep] (c) 3/5 ÷ 4/5 = 3/5 × 5/4 = (3 × (5 ÷ 5))/((5 ÷ 5) × 4) = 3/4.
[WorkedStep] (d) Dividing by 5/3, which is greater than 1, should make 4/9 smaller. 4/9 ÷ 5/3 = 4/9 × 3/5.
[WorkedStep] = (4 × (3 ÷ 3))/((9 ÷ 3) × 5) = 4/15.
