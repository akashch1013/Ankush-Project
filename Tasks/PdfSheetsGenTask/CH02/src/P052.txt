Practice 2C Question 3
[Question] Evaluate and give answers in simplest form.
[Question] (a)  3/7 × 2/3 + 4/7
[Question] (b)  4/5 − 2/3 ÷ 4/8
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a) The calculation is 3/7 × 2/3 + 4/7. Do multiplication before addition:
[WorkedStep] 3/7 × 2/3 = ((3 ÷ 3) × 2)/(7 × (3 ÷ 3)) = 2/7.
[WorkedStep] Now add: 2/7 + 4/7 = (2 + 4)/7 = 6/7.
[WorkedStep] (b) The calculation is 4/5 − 2/3 ÷ 4/8. Do division before subtraction:
[WorkedStep] 2/3 ÷ 4/8 = 2/3 × 8/4 = (2 × (8 ÷ 4))/(3 × (4 ÷ 4)) = 4/3.
[WorkedStep] Now calculate 4/5 − 4/3. Give both fractions denominator 15:
[WorkedStep] 4/5 = (4 × 3)/(5 × 3) = 12/15; 4/3 = (4 × 5)/(3 × 5) = 20/15.
[WorkedStep] We now have 12/15 − 20/15. Taking away 12/15 gets us to 0. There is still 8/15 to take away, so the answer goes below 0.
[WorkedStep] 12/15 − 20/15 = (12 - 20)/15 = −8/15.
[WorkedStep] The minus sign in −8/15 means the answer is 8/15 below zero. Check: −8/15 + 20/15 = 12/15.
