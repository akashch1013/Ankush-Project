## Practice 2I
Practice 2I Question 1
[Question] Use a calculator. Give answers to 2 decimal places where necessary, keeping unrounded values until the final step.
[Question] (a)  2.16 ÷ 6 − 0.04
[Question] (b)  391.4 − 1.26 ÷ 3
[Question] (c)  1.14 + 3.86 ÷ 6
[Question] (d)  77.49 ÷ (2 + 3)
[Question] (e)  4.68 × (8 ÷ 2) − 11.6
[Question] (f)  (8.09 − 1.2456) + 2.17 ÷ 3
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a)  2.16 ÷ 6 = 0.36; 0.36 − 0.04 = 0.32.
[WorkedStep] (b)  1.26 ÷ 3 = 0.42; 391.40 − 0.42 = 390.98.
[WorkedStep] (c)  3.86 ÷ 6 = 0.643333…; 1.14 + 0.643333… = 1.783333… ≈ 1.78.
[WorkedStep] (d) Brackets first: 2 + 3 = 5. Then 77.49 ÷ 5 = 15.498. Keep 15.49; the next digit is 8, so round up to 15.50. Keep the zero for 2 decimal places.
[WorkedStep] (e)  8 ÷ 2 = 4; 4.68 × 4 = 18.72; 18.72 − 11.60 = 7.12.
[WorkedStep] (f)  8.09 − 1.2456 = 6.8444. Also 2.17 ÷ 3 = 0.723333… . Total = 7.567733… ≈ 7.57.
