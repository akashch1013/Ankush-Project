## Comparing Two Quantities by Percentage
Nurul and Linda each save $300 a month. Does that mean each saves the same percentage of her income? We need their incomes to answer that.
Nurul earns $3000 a month; Linda earns $600. Compare Nurul’s $300 with her $3000, and Linda’s $300 with her $600.
In the diagram, each full bar represents 100% of that person’s income. The bars have the same drawn length, but they represent different amounts of money: $3000 for Nurul and $600 for Linda. The shaded part of each bar represents the $300 saved.

Nurul: 300/3000 × 100% = 1/10 × 100% = 10%.
Linda: 300/600 × 100% = 1/2 × 100% = 50%.
Linda saves the larger percentage: 50% > 10%. The same $300 is half of Linda’s $600, but only one tenth of Nurul’s $3000. This compares their savings with their incomes; it does not tell us whose expenses make saving easier.
