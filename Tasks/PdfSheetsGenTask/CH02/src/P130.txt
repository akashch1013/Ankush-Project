Practice 2J Question 3
[Question] The box stands for a whole number and 32/(□) = 20%. Is □ (a) more than 100, (b) less than 100, (c) 100, or (d) none of these?
Check your method
[WorkedStep] Start with the percentage: 20% = 20/100 = 1/5. We need 32/(□) to equal 1/5.
[WorkedStep] To turn the numerator 1 into 32, multiply by 32. Multiply the denominator 5 by 32 too: 1/5 = (1 × 32)/(5 × 32) = 32/160.
[WorkedStep] The missing number is 160. Since 160 > 100, choose (a), more than 100. Check: 32/160 = (32 ÷ 32)/(160 ÷ 32) = 1/5 = 20%.
Practice 2J Question 4
[Question] A tea shop offers 40% less sugar in its drinks. Express this percentage.
[Question] (a)  as a fraction in simplest form
[Question] (b)  as a decimal
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a)  40% = 40/100 = (40 ÷ 20)/(100 ÷ 20) = 2/5.
[WorkedStep] (b)  40% = 40 ÷ 100 = 0.4.
The question asks for the reduction, 40%. If we also want the sugar left, subtract from 100%: 100% − 40% = 60% of the usual amount.
Practice 2J Question 5
[Question] In a 2011 statement, 88.6% of Singapore’s population used public transport. Express this percentage.
[Question] (a)  as a fraction in simplest form
[Question] (b)  as a decimal
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a)  88.6% = 88.6/100 = (88.6 × 10)/(100 × 10) = 886/1000.
[WorkedStep] Divide top and bottom by 2: 886/1000 = (886 ÷ 2)/(1000 ÷ 2) = 443/500.
[WorkedStep] (b)  88.6% = 88.6 ÷ 100 = 0.886.
