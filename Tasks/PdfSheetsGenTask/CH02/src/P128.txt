Example 34
[Question] Amy buys a loaf of bread with a promotional label saying “extra 25%”.
[Question] (a)  What does “extra 25%” mean?
[Question] (b)  Express 25% as a fraction in simplest form.
Let’s work it through
[WorkedStep] (a) “Extra 25%” means the shop adds an amount equal to 25% of the original loaf. In the diagram, the full gold bar represents 1 original loaf. It is divided into 4 equal parts. The teal part is the extra bread.
[WorkedStep] (b) Write 25% as a fraction: 25% = 25/100 = (25 ÷ 25)/(100 ÷ 25) = 1/4. So the extra bread is 1/4 of the original loaf.
[WorkedStep] The original loaf contains 4 quarters. Add the extra quarter: 4/4 + 1/4 = 5/4 = 1 1/4 times the original amount.
[WorkedStep] In percentages, the new total is 100% + 25% = 125% of the original loaf. The 25% labels only the added bread; 125% labels all the bread in the new loaf.

Your turn after Example 34
[Question] A washing-powder packet claims “10% more powder”. Express 10% as a fraction in simplest form.
Check your method
[WorkedStep] 10% = 10/100 = (10 ÷ 10)/(100 ÷ 10) = 1/10. So the extra powder is 1/10 of the usual amount. The new total is 10/10 + 1/10 = 11/10 = 1 1/10 times the usual amount.
