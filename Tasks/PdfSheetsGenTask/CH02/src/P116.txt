## Combined Operations
When a calculation has several operations, we need an agreed order. First calculate inside brackets. Then calculate powers or roots, if there are any. Next do multiplication and division from left to right. Finally do addition and subtraction from left to right.
For example, 12 ÷ 3 × 2 starts with division because ÷ comes first from the left: 12 ÷ 3 × 2 = 4 × 2 = 8. Do not multiply 3 × 2 first.
Similarly, 10 − 4 + 2 = 6 + 2 = 8. Here subtraction comes before addition because it is first from the left. Brackets can change the order: 10 − (4 + 2) = 10 − 6 = 4. Copy every bracket when you use a calculator.
Example 27
[Question] Evaluate.
[Question] (a)  3.5 + 2 × 1.4
[Question] (b)  −1.75 + 15.6 ÷ 5
[Question] (c)  12.7 × 2 − 4.8 ÷ 6
[Question] (d)  (72.5 − 66.2) × 4
Let’s work it through
[WorkedStep] (a) In 3.5 + 2 × 1.4, do the multiplication first: 2 × 1.4 = 2.8. Put 2.8 in place of 2 × 1.4:
[WorkedStep] 3.5 + 2 × 1.4 = 3.5 + 2.8 = 6.3.
[WorkedStep] A quick estimate uses 3.5 ≈ 4 and 1.4 ≈ 1: 4 + 2 × 1 = 4 + 2 = 6. Our answer 6.3 is close.
[WorkedStep] (b) In −1.75 + 15.6 ÷ 5, do the division first: 15.6 ÷ 5 = 3.12. Then −1.75 + 3.12 = 3.12 − 1.75 = 1.37. So −1.75 + 15.6 ÷ 5 = 1.37.
[WorkedStep] (c) First find 12.7 × 2 = 25.4 and 4.8 ÷ 6 = 0.8. Put those answers in place of the multiplication and division:
[WorkedStep] 12.7 × 2 − 4.8 ÷ 6 = 25.4 − 0.8 = 24.6.
[WorkedStep] (d) The brackets come first: 72.5 − 66.2 = 6.3. So (72.5 − 66.2) × 4 = 6.3 × 4 = 25.2.
