# Chapter 2 Key Ideas
We have written amounts as fractions, decimals and percentages. For example, 1/2 = 0.5 = 50%: three ways to name the same half. Before the revision, let’s bring the main methods together with numbers you can follow.
## Fractions
For 2 1/4 + 1 1/2, first give the fraction parts the same denominator: 1/2 = (1 × 2)/(2 × 2) = 2/4.
Add the whole-number parts, 2 + 1 = 3, and the fraction parts, 1/4 + 2/4 = 3/4. So 2 1/4 + 1 1/2 = 3 3/4.
For 4 1/3 − 2 1/6, change 1/3 to sixths: 1/3 = (1 × 2)/(3 × 2) = 2/6. Now subtract the whole-number parts, 4 − 2 = 2, and the fraction parts, 2/6 − 1/6 = 1/6.
Thus 4 1/3 − 2 1/6 = 4 2/6 − 2 1/6 = 2 1/6.
For multiplication, multiply the top numbers and multiply the bottom numbers. Here is an example:
5/6 × 7/8 = (5 × 7)/(6 × 8) = 35/48.
For division, keep the first fraction unchanged and multiply by the reciprocal of the second fraction. In 4/9 ÷ 2/3, keep 4/9 and turn 2/3 into 3/2:
4/9 ÷ 2/3 = 4/9 × 3/2 = (2 × 1)/(3 × 1) = 2/3. Here we divided 4 and 2 by 2, and 3 and 9 by 3.
