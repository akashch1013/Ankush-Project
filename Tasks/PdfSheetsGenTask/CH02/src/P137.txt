Example 35
[Question] Convert each fraction to a percentage.
[Question] (a)  1/2
[Question] (b)  1 3/4
Let’s work it through
[WorkedStep] (a)  One whole is 100%. Split it into 2 equal halves: 100% ÷ 2 = 50%. So 1/2 = 50%, or 1/2 × 100% = 50%.
[WorkedStep] (b) First change 1 3/4 to an improper fraction:
[WorkedStep] 1 3/4 = (1 × 4 + 3)/4 = 7/4.
[WorkedStep] Now multiply by 100%: 7/4 × 100% = 7 × (100 ÷ 4)% = 7 × 25% = 175%.
[WorkedStep] Check: 1 < 1 3/4 < 2, and 100% < 175% < 200%.
Your turn after Example 35
[Question] Convert each fraction to a percentage.
[Question] (a)  1/5
[Question] (b)  2 1/8
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a)  1/5 × 100% = (100 ÷ 5)% = 20%.
[WorkedStep] (b) Change the mixed number first: 2 1/8 = (2 × 8 + 1)/8 = 17/8.
[WorkedStep] Then 17/8 × 100% = 17 × (100 ÷ 8)% = 17 × 12.5% = 212.5%, or 212 1/2%.
[WorkedStep] A percentage can have a decimal or fraction part, as 212.5% does here.

Example 36
[Question] Convert each decimal to a percentage.
[Question] (a)  0.8
[Question] (b)  1.15
Let’s work it through
[WorkedStep] (a) 0.8 means 8 tenths: 0.8 = 8/10. Write it in hundredths: 8/10 = (8 × 10)/(10 × 10) = 80/100. So 0.8 = 80%.
[WorkedStep] The shorter calculation is 0.8 × 100% = 80%.
[WorkedStep] (b) 1.15 means 1 whole and 15 hundredths. One whole is 100 hundredths, so 1.15 = (100 + 15)/100 = 115/100 = 115%.
[WorkedStep] The shorter calculation is 1.15 × 100% = 115%. The percentage is above 100% because 1.15 is above 1.
Your turn after Example 36
[Question] Convert each decimal to a percentage.
[Question] (a)  0.06
[Question] (b)  3.2
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a)  0.06 × 100% = 6%. Six hundredths is six out of a hundred.
[WorkedStep] (b)  3.2 × 100% = 320%. The value is more than three wholes, so the percentage is more than 300%.
