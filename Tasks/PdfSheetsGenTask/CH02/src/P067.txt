## Practice 2D
Practice 2D Question 1
[Question] Convert the fractions to decimals.
[Question] (a)  1/4
[Question] (b)  6/5
[Question] (c)  103/100
[Question] (d)  1 12/60
[Question] (e)  3 9/25
[Question] (f)  80/125
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a) We can make hundredths directly: 1/4 = (1 × 25)/(4 × 25) = 25/100 = 0.25.
[WorkedStep] (b) 6/5 = (6 × 2)/(5 × 2) = 12/10 = 1.2.
[WorkedStep] (c) Separate 100 hundredths from the 103 hundredths: 103/100 = 100/100 + 3/100 = 1 + 0.03 = 1.03. The zero shows that there are no tenths.
[WorkedStep] (d) 12/60 = (12 ÷ 12)/(60 ÷ 12) = 1/5 = (1 × 2)/(5 × 2) = 2/10 = 0.2. So 1 12/60 = 1 + 0.2 = 1.2.
[WorkedStep] (e)  Change the fractional part to hundredths: 9/25 = (9 × 4)/(25 × 4) = 36/100 = 0.36. Then 3 9/25 = 3.36.
[WorkedStep] (f)  80/125 = (80 ÷ 5)/(125 ÷ 5) = 16/25 = (16 × 4)/(25 × 4) = 64/100 = 0.64.
