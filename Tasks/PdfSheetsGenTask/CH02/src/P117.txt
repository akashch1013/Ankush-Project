Example 28
[Question] One notebook costs $1.50 and one ruler costs $1.20. Raj buys 5 notebooks and 4 rulers. Find his total cost.
Let’s work it through
[WorkedStep] One notebook costs $1.50. Five notebooks cost 5 lots of $1.50: 5 × $1.50 = $7.50.
[WorkedStep] One ruler costs $1.20. Four rulers cost 4 lots of $1.20: 4 × $1.20 = $4.80.
[WorkedStep] Add the notebook cost and the ruler cost: $7.50 + $4.80 = $12.30. Raj pays $12.30 altogether.
[WorkedStep] We can combine the steps in one expression: 5 × $1.50 + 4 × $1.20 = $7.50 + $4.80 = $12.30. The multiplications find the two costs; the addition finds the total.
