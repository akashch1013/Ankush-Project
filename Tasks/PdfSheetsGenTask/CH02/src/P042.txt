[WorkedStep] (b) Now calculate 6/11 × 22/30. Multiplying directly gives (6 × 22)/(11 × 30) = 132/330. There is a way to keep the numbers smaller: simplify before multiplying.
[WorkedStep] Write the multiplication as one fraction: (6 × 22)/(11 × 30).
[WorkedStep] Divide the top factor 6 and the bottom factor 30 by 6: 6 ÷ 6 = 1 and 30 ÷ 6 = 5. The other factors, 22 and 11, stay unchanged:
[WorkedStep] (6 × 22)/(11 × 30) = (1 × 22)/(11 × 5).
[WorkedStep] Now divide the top factor 22 and the bottom factor 11 by 11: 22 ÷ 11 = 2 and 11 ÷ 11 = 1:
[WorkedStep] (1 × 22)/(11 × 5) = (1 × 2)/(1 × 5) = 2/5.
[WorkedStep] This is called cancellation. We divided the numerator and denominator by the same number, so the fraction kept the same value. Each cancelled number was a factor, meaning a number being multiplied. This method does not let us cross out numbers that are being added.
[WorkedStep] We can also simplify 22/30 first: 22/30 = (22 ÷ 2)/(30 ÷ 2) = 11/15. Then 6/11 × 11/15 = (6 × 1)/(1 × 15) = 6/15 = (6 ÷ 3)/(15 ÷ 3) = 2/5.
