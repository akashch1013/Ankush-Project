[SceneHeading] Scene CH02 13B The loading table tips
[ProductionLabel] Production instructions are non-printing. Exact reader text is assigned beneath each visual beat.
[ProductionNote] View. Medium across the circular two-tray table from the entrance side: packing bench behind-left, trolley near the lower tray and Return Walk door clear.
[ProductionNote] Cast and positions. The broad helper stands at the outer rim beside the entrance-side tray, within easy reach of the final pack and clear of the trolley handle. Tara is beside its brake, staff right and left hand available. Ahana stands at the trolley handle, Navya opposite the two trays and Vihaan clear beside the packing bench. Tez stays behind the children, outside the wheel and tray arcs.
[ProductionNote] Props and state progression. The packing bench and Return Walk door remain near the entrance. The original cup drawing is clipped above the bench until its explicit removal for dispatch. The upper carrier has sixteen cup places; the separate lower shelf takes one snack tray. The round two-tray loading table is a separate piece of equipment. The crooked cart is parked against the near-left wall clear of both doorways. Ahana’s portable lamp rests at the bench’s entrance corner. All forty identical sealed packs have been loaded onto the two matching table trays according to a wrong card: 25 on the entrance-side tray, 15 on the far tray, both at equal distance from the centre. The brake was already applied; it restrains turning, but unequal loading still tips the supported tray assembly. The trolley’s lower tray remains empty.
[ProductionNote] Planning requirements. Tara catches and holds the existing brake; do not invent an earlier release. Use the rotation latch and squeeze-operated tilt damper defined in Location CH02 L03. Tara’s squeeze checks the sudden dip while permitting slow settling as the loads are equalised; the rotation latch stays engaged. Keep full drinks upright while Ahana moves the trolley clear.
