## Practice 2C
Practice 2C Question 1
[Question] Multiply and give answers in simplest form.
[Question] (a)  1/3 × 6/7
[Question] (b)  2/7 × 6/7
[Question] (c)  3/5 × 7/8
[Question] (d)  3/4 × 7/12
[Question] (e)  2/13 × 26/39
[Question] (f)  5/11 × 55/77
[Question] (g)  2/3 × 6/14
[Question] (h)  2/9 × 3/10
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a) Let’s keep the numbers small: divide 6 and 3 by 3 before multiplying. 1/3 × 6/7 = (1 × (6 ÷ 3))/((3 ÷ 3) × 7).
[WorkedStep] = (1 × 2)/(1 × 7) = 2/7.
[WorkedStep] (b)  2/7 × 6/7 = (2 × 6)/(7 × 7) = 12/49.
[WorkedStep] (c)  3/5 × 7/8 = (3 × 7)/(5 × 8) = 21/40.
[WorkedStep] (d) 3/4 × 7/12 = ((3 ÷ 3) × 7)/(4 × (12 ÷ 3)) = (1 × 7)/(4 × 4) = 7/16.
[WorkedStep] (e) 2/13 × 26/39 = (2 × (26 ÷ 13))/((13 ÷ 13) × 39).
[WorkedStep] = (2 × 2)/(1 × 39) = 4/39.
[WorkedStep] (f) 5/11 × 55/77 = (5 × (55 ÷ 11))/((11 ÷ 11) × 77).
[WorkedStep] = (5 × 5)/(1 × 77) = 25/77.
[WorkedStep] (g) Divide 6 and 3 by 3, then 2 and 14 by 2: 2/3 × 6/14 = (2 × 2)/(1 × 14) = (1 × 2)/(1 × 7) = 2/7.
[WorkedStep] (h) Divide 2 and 10 by 2, and 3 and 9 by 3: 2/9 × 3/10 = (1 × 1)/(3 × 5) = 1/15.
