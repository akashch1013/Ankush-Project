[ProductionNote] Props and state progression. Equal-capacity recipe jugs occupy the far counter; the large juice urn and the separate one-litre reserve bottle occupy the near wet end. The ingredient scale and records are at the dry end, away from the pour. The lamp above-left is fixed. Ahana’s portable lamp remains at the packing bench.
