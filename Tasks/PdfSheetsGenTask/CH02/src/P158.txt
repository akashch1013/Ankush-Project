Revision Question 5
[Question] Evaluate and give answers in simplest form.
[Question] (a)  3 1/3 + 2 2/5
[Question] (b)  4 5/6 + 5 3/4
[Question] (c)  1 1/24 + 5 5/8
[Question] (d)  3 2/9 + 1 5/12
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a)  Use denominator 15: 1/3 = (1 × 5)/(3 × 5) = 5/15 and 2/5 = (2 × 3)/(5 × 3) = 6/15.
[WorkedStep] 3 1/3 + 2 2/5 = 5 + 5/15 + 6/15 = 5 + 11/15.
[WorkedStep] Answer: 5 11/15.
[WorkedStep] (b)  Use denominator 12: 5/6 = (5 × 2)/(6 × 2) = 10/12 and 3/4 = (3 × 3)/(4 × 3) = 9/12.
[WorkedStep] 4 5/6 + 5 3/4 = 9 + 10/12 + 9/12 = 9 + 19/12.
[WorkedStep] 19/12 = 1 7/12, so 9 + 1 7/12 = 10 7/12.
[WorkedStep] (c)  Use denominator 24: 1/24 and 5/8 = (5 × 3)/(8 × 3) = 15/24.
[WorkedStep] 1 1/24 + 5 5/8 = 6 + 1/24 + 15/24 = 6 + 16/24.
[WorkedStep] Simplify: 16/24 = (16 ÷ 8)/(24 ÷ 8) = 2/3.
[WorkedStep] Answer: 6 2/3.
[WorkedStep] (d)  Use denominator 36: 2/9 = (2 × 4)/(9 × 4) = 8/36 and 5/12 = (5 × 3)/(12 × 3) = 15/36.
[WorkedStep] 3 2/9 + 1 5/12 = 4 + 8/36 + 15/36 = 4 + 23/36.
[WorkedStep] Answer: 4 23/36.
