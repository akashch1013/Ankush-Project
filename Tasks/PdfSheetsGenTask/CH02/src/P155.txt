## Decimals and Percentages
To change a fraction to a decimal, divide the numerator by the denominator. For 3/8, divide 3 by 8:
3/8 = 3 ÷ 8 = 0.375.
For a mixed number, you can first change it to an improper fraction: 2 1/10 = (2 × 10 + 1)/10 = 21/10 = 21 ÷ 10 = 2.1.
To change a decimal to a fraction, use its place value. The number 0.35 means 35 hundredths, so write 35 over 100, then simplify:
0.35 = 35/100 = (35 ÷ 5)/(100 ÷ 5) = 7/20
5/8 = 5 ÷ 8 = 0.625
2.3 = 2 3/10
In a mixed calculation, do brackets first, then powers and roots, then multiplication and division from left to right, and finally addition and subtraction from left to right. For example, 3.5 + 2 × 1.4 = 3.5 + 2.8 = 6.3.
For addition and subtraction, line up matching place values: 2.3 + 0.45 becomes 2.30 + 0.45 = 2.75. Check by subtracting: 2.75 − 0.45 = 2.30.

To express one quantity as a percentage of another, put the first quantity on top and the quantity after “of” underneath. Match the units, then multiply by 100%.
For example, 45 cm as a percentage of 1.2 m needs 1.2 m = 120 cm first. Then 45/120 × 100% = 37.5%.
A greater percentage need not mean more money or more objects when the totals differ. For example, 50% of 10 is 5, but 20% of 100 is 20. Always read the amount after “of”.
