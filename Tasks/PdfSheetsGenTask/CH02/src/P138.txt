## Practice 2K
Practice 2K Question 1
[Question] Convert each fraction to a percentage.
[Question] (a)  4/5
[Question] (b)  2/3
[Question] (c)  1/8
[Question] (d)  1 5/6
[Question] (e)  10/3
[Question] (f)  25/4
[Question] (g)  13/100
[Question] (h)  1 1/100
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a) 4/5 × 100% = 400/5% = 80%.
[WorkedStep] (b) 2/3 × 100% = 200/3%. Now 200 ÷ 3 = 66 remainder 2, giving exactly 66 2/3%.
[WorkedStep] Keep the fraction: 66.666…% never stops, and a rounded decimal would no longer be exact.
[WorkedStep] (c) 1/8 × 100% = 100/8% = 12.5%, or 12 1/2%.
[WorkedStep] (d) 1 5/6 = (1 × 6 + 5)/6 = 11/6. Then 11/6 × 100% = 1100/6% = 183 2/6% = 183 1/3%.
[WorkedStep] (e) 10/3 × 100% = 1000/3% = 333 1/3%.
[WorkedStep] (f) 25/4 × 100% = 2500/4% = 625%.
[WorkedStep] (g) 13/100 × 100% = 13%.
[WorkedStep] (h) 1 1/100 = 101/100, so 101/100 × 100% = 101%.
