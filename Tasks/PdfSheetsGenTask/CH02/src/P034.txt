## Practice 2B
An estimate uses easy numbers near the given numbers. For example, 4 2/3 is near 5 and 2 1/3 is near 2, so their sum is about 5 + 2 = 7. Then calculate the exact answer. Different sensible estimates are possible.
Practice 2B Question 1
[Question] Evaluate. Give answers in simplest form.
[Question] (a)  2 1/4 + 1/4
[Question] (b)  3 5/8 + 2 7/8
[Question] (c)  1 6/19 + 2 13/19
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a) 2 1/4 + 1/4 = 2 + (1 + 1)/4 = 2 2/4 = 2 1/2.
[WorkedStep] (b) 3 5/8 + 2 7/8 = 5 + (5 + 7)/8 = 5 + 12/8.
[WorkedStep] Regroup 12/8 = 1 4/8 = 1 1/2, so the total is 5 + 1 1/2 = 6 1/2.
[WorkedStep] (c) 1 6/19 + 2 13/19 = 3 + (6 + 13)/19 = 3 + 1 = 4.
Practice 2B Question 2
[Question] Estimate first, then find the actual value. Give answers in simplest form.
[Question] (a)  4 2/3 + 2 1/3
[Question] (b)  3 1/4 + 1 3/4
[Question] (c)  6 1/6 + 2 5/6
Check your method
[WorkedStep] (a) Estimate: 5 + 2 = 7.
[WorkedStep] Actual value: 4 2/3 + 2 1/3 = 6 + (2 + 1)/3 = 6 + 1 = 7.
[WorkedStep] (b) Estimate: 3 + 2 = 5.
[WorkedStep] Actual value: 3 1/4 + 1 3/4 = 4 + (1 + 3)/4 = 4 + 1 = 5.
[WorkedStep] (c) Estimate: 6 + 3 = 9.
[WorkedStep] Actual value: 6 1/6 + 2 5/6 = 8 + (1 + 5)/6 = 8 + 1 = 9.
