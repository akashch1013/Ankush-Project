[SceneHeading] Scene CH02 11C Tez’s clean-up plan
[ProductionLabel] Production instructions are non-printing. Exact reader text is assigned beneath each visual beat.
[ProductionNote] View. Low medium beside the parked trolley at the packing bench; right-counter end is beyond it and Return Walk doorway remains near-left.
[ProductionNote] Props and state progression. The packing bench and Return Walk door remain near the entrance. The original cup drawing is clipped above the bench until its explicit removal for dispatch. The upper carrier has sixteen cup places; the separate lower shelf takes one snack tray. The round two-tray loading table is a separate piece of equipment. The crooked cart is parked against the near-left wall clear of both doorways. Ahana’s portable lamp rests at the bench’s entrance corner. Sixteen cups are full in the upper carrier. The snack tray is empty. Navya has left the record pencil on the dry drink counter. No spill has actually occurred. Before this view, the broad helper returns the same trolley from the drink counter to the packing bench, using both hands on its handle. The sixteen full cups stay upright in their slots; their caps are added later in CH02 12C C. The trio and Tara walk back along the clear lane; Tez follows clear of the wheels. Show the completed arrival, not simultaneous travel and conversation.
[ProductionNote] Planning requirements. This joke does not authorise Tez to lick food equipment, carry a cloth in human hands or drink from the first sixteen cups.
