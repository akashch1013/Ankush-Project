[PanelizationLabel] ORIGINAL PANEL CONTENT
Let’s compare three divisions with the same starting number, 6.
6 ÷ 1/2 = 12. Dividing by 1/2, a positive fraction below 1, gives a number greater than 6.
6 ÷ 3/2 = 6 × 2/3 = 4. Dividing by 3/2, a fraction greater than 1, gives a number smaller than 6.
6 ÷ 2/2 = 6 ÷ 1 = 6. Dividing by 1 leaves the number unchanged.
These size checks apply to positive starting amounts. Read the fraction you divide by before deciding whether the answer should be bigger or smaller.
Our answer 15/32 in Example 9 was part of a group. But if a question asks how many full bottles we can fill, a part-filled bottle does not count as full. Watch for that word in the next question.
