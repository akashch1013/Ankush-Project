Check your method
All the colours together make all the sweets: 150/150 = 1 whole = 100%. So the percentages add to 100% before rounding.
Our unfinished sample shows only 10 green sweets, so it does not yet total 100%. Once every count is entered, rounding may make the displayed percentages total 99.99% or 100.01%. Check the counts and the unrounded values before declaring a sweet missing!

Why multiply a fraction by 100%? One whole is 100%, so half a whole is 1/2 × 100% = 50%. Three quarters of a whole is 3/4 × 100% = 75%.
Keep the % sign with 50: 1/2 = 50%, because 50% means 50/100. The number 50 on its own would mean 50 wholes!
