Practice 2E Question 7
[Question] Fresh Fruit Stall sells 3 pears for $2.00. Lucky Fruit Stall sells the same type of pears at 1 for $0.70. Which stall would you choose? Explain.
Check your method
[WorkedStep] Compare the same number of pears. Three at Lucky cost 3 × $0.70 = $2.10. Three at Fresh cost $2.00.
[WorkedStep] Choose Fresh Fruit Stall. For the same 3 pears, you save $2.10 − $2.00 = $0.10.
