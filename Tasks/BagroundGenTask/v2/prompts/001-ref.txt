Asset type: one clean environment illustration with no characters, landscape 16:9.
Input images (style and world references, not layouts):
- Image 1: an earlier concept sheet of this same Lantern Street at night. Take from it the world's look: the deep navy-and-gold palette, the weathered blue-grey stone and navy-and-brass construction, the navy banners with gold stars and moons, the brass wall lanterns, the rich tall architecture with greenery, the reflective paving, the moonlit starry sky, the rendering finish and the detail density. Camera: standing at the Entry Court threshold at a child's eye height (about 1.2 m), looking straight along Lantern Street towards Food Street. Natural wide lens, no fisheye. Generous open paving fills the lower third of the frame. Left and right below are as seen from this camera.

Place: Lantern Street is a broad lane of pale blue-grey dressed stone paving, wide enough for a group of children and a handcart to pass together. It runs straight ahead, then bends gently to the left in the far distance. Both sides are lined with blue-painted timber stall walls with brass corner straps and indigo fabric valances, beneath tall multi-storey stone fair architecture with balconies, cornices, ivy and towers, as in Image 1. Tall indigo-and-brass arches span the lane at intervals, each carrying hanging lantern fittings. Repeated warm brass wall lamps along both sides lead the eye into the depth.

State and light: night. Every lantern on the overhead arches is unlit. The moonline is unlit. The five indicator lamps are dark. The Food Street shutters are closed. Only the fixed warm wall and work lamps glow, making amber pools at the lift, along the bench and in the mirror alcove; elsewhere the street is lit by cool full-moon light, atmospheric rather than fully bright. Keep kit parts, mirror tiles, cube stacks and controller details too small to read rather than drawing any solved arrangement.

Art style: match the rendering, palette, contrast and detail density of Images 1 and 2. High-end fantasy environment concept art that blends photographic realism with storybook illustration. Materials have near-photographic conviction: polished and cast brass with bright specular edges and a light patina; navy-blue painted timber and iron with rivets, bolt heads, edge banding and fine wear; weathered blue-grey dressed stone with chipped edges; clear lantern glass; deep indigo banners with gold-embroidered eight-pointed stars, crescent moons and gold fringe. Forms stay clean, idealised and orderly. A night palette of deep ultramarine, cobalt and navy against warm amber, honey-gold and brass: strong warm-cool contrast, saturated but natural, never neon. Warm lanterns glow with soft bloom; cool full-moon light fills the shadows; the polished, slightly wet-looking paving mirrors the lamps in long golden reflections. Deep, rich shadows that keep readable detail. A deep blue starry sky with soft moonlit clouds and exactly one large, bright full moon. Dense craftsmanship on frames, walls and upper edges; the floor and working surfaces stay open, clean and readable. Crisp focus throughout; only a gentle moonlit haze in the far distance.
