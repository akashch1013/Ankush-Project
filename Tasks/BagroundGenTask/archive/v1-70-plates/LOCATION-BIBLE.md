## 1. Look (all plates)

**World and materials:**
- **World:** ChandRush, a travelling full-moon fair, set up inside Mumbai for one night.
- **Materials:** blue-painted timber stalls, pale stone paving, indigo frames and arches, brushed brass, and selected gold motifs (full-moon circles, compass stars). There are **no crescent emblems** and no religious motifs.

**Light:**
- It is the same full-moon night throughout.
- Warm fixed work lamps light the work areas; cool moonlight edges the roofs and canopy gaps.
- Any visible sky moon is **one complete full moon**, never a crescent.
- The main lantern arches stay **dark** until scene 10I.

**Rendering:**
- Cinematic, painterly graphic-novel environments with a soft stylised-3D finish, matching the approved location pack and the Opening pages.
- **Grandeur comes from scale, depth and light**, not dense tiny machinery.

**Plates are empty sets:**
- No people, no Tez, no wooden helpers, crews or crowds. They are added when panels are made.
- Fixed set dressing stated by the scene **is** included: the parked street cart, supply boxes, cartons, tiles, tray and crate, and the Food Street cart and bowls.

**No lettering:**
- Label plates, tick marks, wheel windows, cards, the readiness board and the packing sheet are **blank**. Exact words and numbers are added later.
- The single exception is the scratched **V** in plate MR-05b; it is a story clue.

**Space for the page:**
- Keep the floor where the scene stages characters clear.
- Keep the top band calm (sky, wall or arch) for captions.

**Distant counts are approximate:** tiles, cubes, ticks and lamps in wide views. Plates that must be exact are flagged in their checks.

## 2. Lantern Street — fixed geography

Directions are as if you walk **in from the Entry Court arch (south) towards Food Street (north)**. Left = west wall, right = east wall. A reverse view swaps the *screen* sides, never the physical sides.

```
                 FAR BEND (curves gently right) — tall closed FOOD STREET SHUTTERS
   WEST (left)                                                      EAST (right)
   mirror ALCOVE  ............ opposite ............  CUBE PLINTH under indigo canopy
   (lamp on its left = south side;                     brass floor COVER at its front-right
    tray + crate beside it, north side)                (south-west) corner, over the moonline
                                                       WALL BOARD (5 lamps) + DOWN-PULL HANDLE
                                                       (south of the plinth)
   blue stalls, closed shutters                        ┌ BENCH (one continuous pale bench) ┐
                                                       │  timing device  (north end)       │
                                                       │  projector kits (centre)          │
   LIFT BAY: shaft + brass gate,                       │  hook rack      (south end)       │
   position strip at its right edge,                   └───────────────────────────────────┘
   controller right of the strip, warm lamp above       moonline = silver track along the
   parked empty street cart (just inside the arch)      paving's RIGHT (east) edge
                 SOUTH: Entry Court archway (where the chapter starts)
   Overhead: tall INDIGO LANTERN ARCHES span the lane at regular intervals.
   Beyond the roofs: night sky, one full moon, distant Mumbai towers and palms.
```

**Bench orientation.** The manuscript describes the bench as seen **from the lane, facing it**: timing at its *left* end, kits in the centre, the rack at its *right* end. Facing the east wall, left is north, so the timing device is at the far (down-lane) end and the hook rack is nearest the entrance. This is a literal reading; it only changes the order in the distant master view. Inside every bench view the order is always **timing · kits · rack**, left to right.

**Lane:**
- a broad pale-paved lane, gently curving right at the far end;
- two-storey stalls of blue-painted timber with closed shutters, indigo frames and brass fittings;
- banners with gold full-moon circles and stars.

**Indigo lantern arches:**
- Tall indigo-painted arches span the lane.
- Each crossbeam carries a row of **lantern projector heads**: blue glass lenses held in brass rings, on brass support brackets.
- They are dark until 10I, then golden.

**Moonline:** a narrow silver track set flush in the paving along its right (east) edge. It runs past the bench and plinth to a **flat square brass cover** at the plinth's front-right corner. It is unlit until 10I, then shines continuously.

**Parked street cart:** an empty wooden handcart parked against the left stall wall just inside the arch, outside the walking route. Its long handle projects into the lane at knee height. It is separate from the supply cart below.

## 3. Goods-lift bay and controller (left wall, near the entrance)

**The shaft:**
- One shaft is recessed into the blue wall. At street level it is closed by a **brass lattice safety gate**.
- Behind the gate is **one small open-cage goods car**: a brass frame with a timber floor.
- A flush **blank brass level mark** is set in the paving at the gate.

**Position strip:**
- A vertical indigo enamel strip runs on the wall at the shaft's right edge.
- Its **equal blank tick marks** run above and below a bolder tick at street level (0).
- A bolder tick far down marks the store (−8); there are eight equal intervals between 0 and −8.

**Controller** (on the paving to the strip's right):
- a waist-high blue-and-brass cabinet with **one warm brass wall lamp** above it;
- **top rail:** a row of card slots, a narrow card shelf, three round brass push buttons with blank caps (START, HOLD, CANCEL LAST CHANGE) and a small dark display window (the last-change window);
- on the cabinet face: **two small red warning lamps**;
- below them, a **hinged lower panel** that opens to show **four equal brass correction wheels** with blank windows and a **blank fixed maintenance plate**.

**Lower store (−8):**
- a guarded landing below the same shaft: pale timber floor, brass safety rail, a **sliding lower gate**, timber store wall, warm hanging lamp, a blank landing mark plate, and a small metal box on the wall;
- the **supply cart**, a wooden handcart loaded with supply boxes, starts here.

There is only ever **one car** and **one supply cart**. Never show a second car or a motion ghost.

## 4. The bench (right wall) — three working areas

**Timing device (bench's left end):**
- On the pale bench, under a warm lamp:
  - a **blue-enamelled brass catch arm** with a small **blue glass lamp** above it (left);
  - a matching **gold-brass catch arm** with a small **gold glass lamp** (right);
  - between them, on a low back panel, a horizontal **beat strip** of evenly spaced blank marks and a small round **counter dial** with a blank face;
  - a **start-card slot** holding one blank card.
- In front, a short **brass track** runs towards the bench edge. At its back end is a small **sliding platform** carrying **one carved wooden bird**, held by both catch arms.
- States: arm down = lamp off; arm raised = lamp lit.
- When both are raised, the platform slides to an **end stop** near the bench edge and the bird **bows**.
- **Do not use timing drums.**

**Projector-kit area (bench centre):**
- **Two open wooden supply boxes** with blank brass label plates: the left one is heaped with **small round blue glass lenses**, the right one with **small brass rings**.
- **Shallow kit boxes lined with indigo padding**.
- One **blank pale sample board** (wood with felt).
- A short **brass lantern-test rail** on the blue wall above, holding empty projector-head brackets.
- Never show a finished projector head or filled kits before 06B.

**Sealed-hook rack (bench's right end):**
- A **brass rack** (two uprights, crossbars) rises against the blue wall above the bench.
- Below it stand **four identical sealed cartons**, each with a blank paper label and an **intact paper quantity seal**.
- Above each carton, a **brass spring clip** holds one blank pale **team card**.
- The rack's **right brass upright** and the pale bench edge anchor every close-up.
- There are no loose hooks, and no V on the rack.

## 5. Square mirror recess (left wall, opposite the plinth)

**The alcove:**
- A shallow alcove in the blue timber wall, with a **blue rear wall**.
- An **open canopy gap** overhead lets in moonlight.
- A **warm wall lamp on its left**.

**The frame:**
- A **shallow square brass frame**, about a metre wide, stands just above the paving.
- It is tilted slightly back towards the canopy gap, and has a fine **inner brass rim**.

**Next to it:** a **quilted indigo padded tray** and a **closed opaque wooden crate** sit on the paving beside the alcove (north side), outside the walking lane.

**Tiles:** 81 equal square mirror tiles, none broken.

**States:**
1. Tiles piled crookedly in the frame, throwing **broken patches of moonlight** on the opposite (east) wall.
2. Frame empty, tiles stacked on the tray.
3. Grid almost complete, one gap.
4. **One flat 9 × 9 grid reflecting one complete full moon**.
5. In restored gold light, a **V scratched in the dust** of the inner rim (11A only).

**Never** show the old floor-mounted square in the street, or a 3 × 27 tile array.

## 6. Cube plinth, readiness board, handle and moonline joint (right side, opposite the mirror)

**The plinth:**
- A **knee-high square plinth** of pale stone with brass edging, under a small **indigo canopy** on slim posts.
- **Four slim brass corner rods** rise from its corners, and a **square brass top frame** rides on them.
- The **lantern cubes** are 64 palm-sized cubes with brass edges and frosted glass.

**Readiness board and handle** (on the blue wall to the plinth's right):
- a panel with **five small round lamps in a row**, each above a **blank brass label plate**; order left to right: LIFT, RHYTHM, SUPPLIES, MIRROR, CUBE;
- beside it, a **tall brass down-pull lever handle**.

**Joint cover:**
- A **flat square brass cover** sits flush in the paving at the plinth's front-right corner, over the moonline joint.
- Under it: two **disconnected silver track ends**, screws, and **black dust** in the screw holes.
- There is no second switch, box or socket. **Never** show the old enclosed cube bay, cube guide drawing or cylindrical socket.

**Cube states:**
1. Thin, uneven towers, with the top frame stuck high.
2. Three solid 4 × 4 layers with a part fourth layer.
3. One solid **4 × 4 × 4** cube with the top frame fastened flush.
4. Restored: the cube glows softly.

## 8. Food Street threshold (scenes 11E–11F)

**The threshold:**
- **Saffron and teal awnings** cover a lane beyond the risen shutters; the shutters roll up into the lintel.

**Fixed layout:**
- **Left:** the recipe and cooking counter, with the hot cooking surface behind it, plus brass serving dials and levers (blank).
- **Right:** a drinks counter with jugs and a tall juice urn.
- **Near entrance, right:** a packing bench with a small **side door** (to Return Walk) and a wheeled trolley holding rows of cups.
- **Central lane:** a **round two-tray loading table**.
- **Far ahead:** the opening to the **Parade Ground**.

**Obstruction (11E–11F):**
- **one crooked wooden food cart** blocks the entrance, with **a bowl trapped under one wheel**;
- other bowls lie fallen nearby;
- a large golden **dosa hangs over a plate far too small for it** on the left counter.

**Light:** warm gold light from restored Lantern Street on the near paving; task lamps inside. There are no cooks or people.
