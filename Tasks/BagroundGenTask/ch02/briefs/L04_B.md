The near seat corner, curved bench arm, paving in front, right stone pier and a section of the blue main leaf appear in the frame. Location and construction The exterior faces the two monumental indigo timber leaves between pale carved-stone piers. Brass celestial ornament, layered blue-and-gold arches, burgundy drapery, the two carved figures, warm lanterns and the round full-moon ornament match the Old Blue Gate master. Seen from outside, the small service door is cut into the left main leaf; the separate equipment exit is farther left, beyond the stone pier. The planter remains left and the blue-and-brass bench remains right. The bench retains the master’s slatted blue seat and back, brass edging, curved brass arms and original legs resting on the stone paving beside the right pier. The near seat corner provides a dry support surface. A clear approach lies directly in front of that corner, with a second floor patch beside the near bench arm.
Visible arrangement The visible main Gate leaf is closed. The bench seat is clear, including a flat patch large enough for a sheet of paper. The paving in front of the near corner is open for a child to bend or sit beside the writing surface. The separate patch beside the arm leaves the bench approach unobstructed. The nearby lantern casts warm light across the seat, with a cool blue rim on the brass and stone.
Appearance Premium semi-realistic painterly 2.5D graphic-novel illustration matching the Old Blue Gate master: dimensional materials, rich indigo timber, warm brass and pale stone; fixed amber lamps with cool full-moon light. The master’s architectural proportions, ornament density, texture scale and light direction remain intact.
