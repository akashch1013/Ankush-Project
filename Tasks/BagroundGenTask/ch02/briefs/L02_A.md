The full front slab edge, cupboard doors, dry work area, filling area, dispenser and plate shelf are visible, with a clear strip of paving below.
Location and construction This is the physical-left counter when looking from the Lantern Street entrance towards the Parade end. Its cream stone slab, indigo cupboard fronts, brass corner plates, blue timber backdrop and fixed warm work lamp match the Food Street master. The dry book area is at the entrance end, the filling area next to it, and the dispenser and cooking area farther along. The front paving gives access to the cupboard and cool counter edge; the working aisle behind the cooking area is clear. One brass tank stands on fixed supports above a single adjustable measuring cup and a separate cooking plate. A narrow inspection window on the tank’s cool-facing lower side reveals its interior above the supply tap while preserving the brass silhouette. The cup has a cleaning hinge on its Parade-facing side, attached to the tank support, a cool outer grip bracket and an adjustable fill stop. A supply tap between tank and cup is reachable from the cool entrance end. A long handle extends towards the cool front edge; its linkage isolates tank feed before opening the cup outlet. At rest the handle is raised and the outlet is shut. A separate heater switch serves the cooking plate behind the guard. The dry plate shelf is beside that plate, outside the heated area. 
Visible arrangement The original recipe book, a two-pan beam balance, seven equal ¼ kg weights and one batter stock tray occupy the dry end. A closed blue-lidded box rests above the cupboard. Two covered filling bowls and five empty sharing trays occupy the separate filling area. One oversized cooked dosa overhangs its small plate; one tiny cooked dosa rests on another plate on the dry shelf. The dispenser tank and cup are empty, the fill stop sits above a lower unlabelled tick, the supply tap is closed and the heater is off. The raised handle and shut cup outlet are visible. The fixed lamp lights the worktop.
Appearance Premium semi-realistic painterly 2.5D graphic-novel illustration matching the Food Street master: dimensional materials, rich indigo timber, warm brass and pale stone; fixed amber lamps with cool full-moon light. The master’s architectural proportions, ornament density, texture scale and light direction remain intact.
