Location and construction This is the physical-right counter when looking from Lantern Street towards the Parade end. Its cream stone slab, indigo timber panels, brass trim, teal glazed vessels and broad brass urn match the Food Street master. The fixed work lamp is above-left in the front-facing counter view. The two recipe jugs stand together on the slab; the urn has a reachable front tap and a clear trolley bay beside it. The dry weighing end lies farther towards the Parade end, beyond the jugs and away from the urn outlet. The recipe jugs have equal capacities and retain the master’s teal silhouettes. Narrow vertical sight windows face the aisle so their liquid levels are visible without changing the overall vessel shapes. The brass urn has a front-facing calibrated sight tube. A low reachable shelf carries the capped reserve bottle; a small transfer jug stands on the dry slab.
Visible arrangement The left recipe jug contains 0.75 L and the right recipe jug 0.7 L, shown on their readable level scales. A small physical recipe card beside them reads ¾ L. The urn’s sight tube reads 3.9 L and its tap is closed. The capped reserve bottle has 1 L capacity and a 0.60 L liquid level. The small transfer jug is empty. The trolley bay beside the urn is clear, and the pale front edge is dry and accessible.
Appearance Premium semi-realistic painterly 2.5D graphic-novel illustration matching the Food Street master: dimensional materials, rich indigo timber, warm brass and pale stone; fixed amber lamps with cool full-moon light. The master’s architectural proportions, ornament density, texture scale and light direction remain intact.
