Camera and framing 4:3 landscape low three-quarter view along the cooking counter’s dry plate shelf from the customer aisle. The camera is just above the shelf surface, high enough to see both dosa tops, and tilted gently down to include the paving below. The entrance end is frame-left. The front lip, cupboard base and lower part of the brass dispenser remain visible.
Location and construction This is the physical-left counter when looking from the Lantern Street entrance towards the Parade end. Its cream stone slab, indigo cupboard fronts, brass corner plates, blue timber backdrop and fixed warm work lamp match the Food Street master. The dry book area is at the entrance end, the filling area next to it, and the dispenser and cooking area farther along. The dry plate shelf stands beside the guarded cooking plate at the height and depth established by the master’s counter. Its cream front lip, indigo supports and cupboard base retain those proportions. The dispenser has one fixed brass tank with a narrow inspection window above its supply tap, one hinged measuring cup, a cool-side long handle and a separate guarded cooking plate. Clear paving extends below the shelf and towards the viewer.
Visible arrangement One oversized cooked dosa overhangs its small plate; one tiny cooked dosa sits on a second plate beside it. Both plates rest fully on the dry shelf. The tank and cup are empty, the portion stop sits above a lower unlabelled tick, the supply tap is closed and the long handle rests raised. The foreground paving and the reachable cool front lip are clear. Warm task light falls across the shelf and floor.
Appearance Premium semi-realistic painterly 2.5D graphic-novel illustration matching the Food Street master: dimensional materials, rich indigo timber, warm brass and pale stone; fixed amber lamps with cool full-moon light. The master’s architectural proportions, ornament density, texture scale and light direction remain intact.
