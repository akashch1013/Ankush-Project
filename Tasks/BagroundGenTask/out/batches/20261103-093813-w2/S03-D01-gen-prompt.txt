Production rules (apply to every plate):
- Frame: one 4:3 landscape scenic image filling the whole canvas edge to edge. No border, title band, panel frame, vignette box or blank margin.
- Lettering: the only lettering allowed is the physical inscriptions listed above, rendered exactly as written, letter for letter, with each minus sign as a true minus sign. No other words, numbers or symbols anywhere. No title, caption, callout, label box, leader line, arrow, legend, watermark or signature: titles and callouts are added later by the production team.
- Reference images: their titles, annotation words, label boxes and arrows only identify places for you. Reproduce none of them.

- Counts and states are exact as stated. Add no extra copies of any counted object and no extra devices, cards, lamps or tiles.
- Unlit means unlit: clear or smoky glass with no flame, no inner glow and no bloom. Lit lamps glow only at their own glass.
