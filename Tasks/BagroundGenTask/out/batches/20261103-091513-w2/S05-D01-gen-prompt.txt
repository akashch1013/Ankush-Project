Look and finish (same for every ChandRush location plate): premium semi-realistic painterly 2.5D graphic-novel illustration that matches the supplied ChandRush masters, a blend of illustration and reality. It is painterly in finish but crisp and precise in drawing. Materials and light have near-photographic conviction while forms stay clean, idealised and orderly, like a high-end storybook. ChandRush is an ancient travelling fair with grand, mystic, magical scale and real architectural detailing: dimensional deep-indigo and ultramarine painted timber with visible grain, joinery, rivets and edge banding; warm polished and cast brass with bright specular edges and a light patina; pale cream stone worktops and pale irregular stone paving with a light wet sheen and crisp reflections; layered fabric canopies; restrained circular celestial ornament (full-moon discs, rings, small eight-point stars) in gold and brass. Palette: ultramarine, cobalt, indigo and navy against amber, honey-gold and brass, with strong warm-cool contrast, saturated but natural, never neon. Light: fixed amber task lamps cast clean, well-defined warm pools, their glow held tight around the lamp glass with no spreading halo; cool full-moon light edges roofs, canopy gaps, stone and brass; deep, rich shadows that keep readable detail. Clarity: clear, dry night air with no haze, fog, mist, smoke or airborne dust. Crisp edges and high micro-contrast. Sharp, clean textures in wood grain, brass and stone, as in a high-resolution photograph. True deep darks and clean colours, never a milky, washed-out or glowing veil over the image, never soft focus. The background stays crisp and only slightly darker with distance. Ornament is dense above eye level and on frames; floors, worktops and mechanisms stay open, clean and readable.
