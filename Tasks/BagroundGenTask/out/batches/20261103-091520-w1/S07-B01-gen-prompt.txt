Clarity: clear, dry night air with no haze, fog, mist, smoke or airborne dust. Crisp edges and high micro-contrast. Sharp, clean textures in wood grain, brass and stone, as in a high-resolution photograph. Production rules (apply to every plate):

- Lettering: the only lettering allowed is the physical inscriptions listed above, rendered exactly as written, letter for letter, with each minus sign as a true minus sign. - Reference images: their titles, annotation words, label boxes and arrows only identify places for you. Reproduce none of them.

- Counts and states are exact as stated. Add no extra copies of any counted object and no extra devices, cards, lamps or tiles.
- Unlit means unlit: clear or smoky glass with no flame, no inner glow and no bloom. Lit lamps glow only at their own glass.
