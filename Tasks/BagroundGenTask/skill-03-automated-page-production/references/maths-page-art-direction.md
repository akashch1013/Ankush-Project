# Maths-page art direction

Apply this guidance when planning and reviewing every maths-focused page, including pages with a detached character aside rather than a story panel. Treat it as the author's carried-forward design preference, within the current manuscript and manual. Use it alongside the full production checklist; it does not replace the 40 checks.

## Character scenery and available space

1. Reserve the complete teaching units, exact lettering and safe reading space first. At full-page scale identify genuinely usable areas to the character's left, right and above. Distinguish these from margins, reading gutters, purposeful separation between solutions and intentional writing/answer space.
2. When a character is required or permitted and the page has usable space, plan the character and a coherent part of the approved setting together. Prefer a setting that extends meaningfully into those regions over a tiny isolated scene or a cutout with a token floor patch. Do not merely acknowledge that space exists; use it or record a concrete page-specific reason why extension would harm readability, source order, continuity or image quality.
3. Reuse more of the approved location image where its view, state and native resolution fit. If the existing image ends too soon, create only the necessary background extension or matching view. Preserve the character's face, apparent age, hair/fur, outfit, gesture and prop ownership unless the source or correction calls for a change. Preserve location identity, perspective, lighting, colours and current repair state.
4. Extend scenery rather than automatically enlarging the entire image and character. Choose a natural fragment of the established wall, rail, paving, arch or other relevant setting; retain believable scale, support and contact shadows. Do not invent a panorama, machinery, inscriptions or story events to fill space. Do not use stretching, mirroring or upscaling as a substitute for real detail. Check the final crop and effective PPI after the extension.
5. Where appropriate, blend only the outer scenery softly into the exact surrounding page colour through an irregular boundary. Keep the character, meaningful props, feet/paws and contact shadows crisp and opaque. Avoid a rectangular remnant, white fringe, abrupt edge, transparent face or floating body. A deliberate frame remains valid when it serves this particular composition; record why.
6. Keep scenery entirely clear of text, equations, diagrams, table cells and continuous working. Keep reading surfaces pale and calm. Do not shrink essential type, break a solution sequence or move material to another page to accommodate scenery. Decorative shapes must not be mistaken for countable mathematical evidence.
7. Compare recent relevant character appearances. Use a fresh, meaningful and child-friendly pose when the authored line/action permits it, rather than repeatedly placing the same stock cutout. Retain a pose when continuity or a narrowly scoped correction requires it.

Inspect the new image immediately, then its exact intended placement with the real lettering, then the actual InDesign export. An image may pass its identity check but still require a composition change after placement.

## Pages with little usable space

Preserve an open teaching composition where a character and setting would crowd the mathematics. A page does not need a character merely because another page has one. Use a restrained ChandRush border, corner or established prop motif if it strengthens the composition and is consistent with the manual and source.

Tara's staff or the Moon Bell can inform an appropriate graphic border or corner motif, using the approved design. Do not imply that a missing Bell has physically returned or that Tara has changed prop ownership. A realistic prop needs believable support and context; a clearly stylised border motif need not sit on a literal floor. Avoid a floating photographic object. Do not add a new quote: preserve only the exact assigned/author-approved aside.

## Corners, borders and physical teaching surfaces

Vary motifs and border treatments within ChandRush's established visual language. On a page whose perimeter has room, make chosen corner details sufficiently visible at normal tablet/laptop size; do not make them so faint or tiny that they have no effect. Keep them clear of reading fields and mathematical diagrams. Avoid decorating every corner automatically or adding clutter to compensate for weak composition.

Use devices other than an open book where suitable: an established fair plaque, supported sign, notice surface or appropriate machine panel. Preserve the manuscript's required surfaces and objects. Choose nearly frontal readable planes for extended mathematics. Lettering must follow the actual surface and margins, remain editable, and avoid bolts, hands, folds and bindings.

If a book is chosen, make its shape visibly recognisable: enough binding, cover edge, page thickness or page curvature to read as a book at normal size. Keep its reading surface calm; do not hide its identity behind a generic pale rectangle. Never distort, shrink or crop the source content to make the device more decorative.

## Evidence in the exported-page review

Use the relevant existing V02 checks: B01–B04 for identity/space/teaching clearance, B07–B08 for surfaces/support, C01–C07 for character/setting/pose/boundary, E03–E04 for real image resolution, and F03/F08/F09 for design, integration and unchanged colours. Record page-specific observations in the normal 40-check record.

For a spacious vignette page, identify which region was extended, the visible coherent setting, how the character is grounded, how the outer edge meets the page and what keeps teaching clear. If space stays open, state its actual reading or composition purpose. These are fields to inspect, not ready-made PASS statements.

Mark NEEDS FIX when the actual export still has a needlessly cramped scenic island, a large unexplained gap beside/above it, repeated unrelated decoration, an invisible book form, ineffective requested corners or scenery that intrudes on teaching. Do not pass design merely because all text is present or the image meets 300 PPI. Correct locally, re-export and inspect again. After fades/extension, check unaffected colours, opacity, seams, crops and neighbouring elements as well as the changed image.
