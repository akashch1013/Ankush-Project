# CH01-P012 — The Bell's two rings and the theft

**Book opening / prologue · V07 page 12 (was V06 p08)**
Location: Entry Court, beside the empty circular Moon Bell stand on its stepped base.

## Story beat

Tara explains why the Bell matters. It must ring twice on every ChandRush night. The first ring opens the fair. The second opens the Moon Path so the fair can fold into its silver wagons and leave before moonset. Then she tells what happened: a tall figure with an old silver ticket and a polished walking stick entered the hidden staff route, stole the Bell and moved through the fair.
