## Story beat

Tez marks the three invitations with a blue volunteer mark. The marks open a small emergency door in the Gate. Inside, the reveal: Tara waits beside an empty stand. The Moon Bell is gone. This page is the hinge from "the fair is broken" to "the Bell was stolen", so the reveal panel is the larger one.
